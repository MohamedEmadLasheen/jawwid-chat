Delete your Jawwid account

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-11-17 · Language: English · العربية

This page explains how to delete your account in Jawwid: Quran Academy, the app of Jawwid Academy, published by Jawwid LLC. You do not need the app to delete your account. Section 10 of the Privacy Policy gives the full detail.

1. How to delete your account

1.1 In the app: Settings → Delete account. You confirm with your password.

1.2 On the web, without the app: open the account deletion form at https://api.chat.jawwid.com/api/v1/account-deletion?lang=en. Enter your sign-in name and your password, tick that you understand that deletion is final, and press "Delete my account for good".

1.3 If you cannot sign in: write to info@jawwid.com from the e-mail address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity before acting.

1.4 Parents and teachers can delete their own accounts. A staff account is closed by the Academy when the staff member leaves.

1.5 Teachers: if a teacher still has a future lesson, an assigned student, or this month's or last month's payroll open, deleting records a deletion request instead. The Academy moves the teacher's students and completes the payroll, and the account is deleted within 30 days of the request at the latest, even if something is still open. The app and the form show the date. Until then the teacher can cancel the request in the app (Settings → Delete account). A teacher with nothing open is deleted at once.

1.6 Deletion is final and cannot be undone.

2. What is deleted at once

2.1 Deletion takes effect immediately; after a teacher's request (clause 1.5), it takes effect when the request is completed, at the latest on the date shown. We delete:

(a) your sign-in name, your password, your devices, your push tokens, any pending reset codes and your own notifications; you are signed out everywhere;

(b) your profile photo and "about" text; your name is replaced by "Deleted user" wherever it appears;

(c) the contact details the Academy's staff hold for you (phone, e-mail and region), and the staff note;

(d) your location prompt and your own contact-matching keys; and

(e) any block you made, and any block made against you.

2.2 For a teacher, we also delete the teacher's phone number, e-mail address, professional profile and certificate files. The payout account is deleted at once, or, if payroll is still open when the account is deleted, as soon as it is closed (Privacy Policy, section 10.4).

3. What is kept, and for how long

3.1 Some records are kept after deletion, because other people rely on them, for the Academy's legal and financial duties, or as evidence. Where the table says "not deleted automatically", no deletion period has been set yet and the record is kept until the Academy decides otherwise; you can ask us about any of them at info@jawwid.com.

3.2 Our service providers keep some records under their own rules, for example delivery records of the e-mails we send. The Privacy Policy (section 10.7) explains each one.
