# Reporting and Complaints Policy

**Version** {{VERSION}} · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](reporting-and-complaints-policy-ar.md)

## 1. About this policy

1.1 This policy explains how Parents, Teachers and Academy staff can report a message, a person or a concern to
Jawwid Academy ("Jawwid", the "Academy", "we"), operated by {{ENTITY_NAME}}; what we do with a report; how
quickly we respond; and how to make a complaint about our service.

1.2 It forms part of the rules of the Service together with the Code of Conduct, the Blocking, Suspension and
Enforcement Policy, the Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy and the Privacy Policy. Words defined in
the Code of Conduct have the same meaning here.

1.3 **Emergencies first.** If anyone, in particular a child, is in immediate danger, call your local emergency
services before doing anything else: {{EMERGENCY_CONTACTS}}. Then tell the Academy.

## 2. Ways to raise a concern

2.1 You can raise a concern in any of these ways:
(a) **Report in the app** a message or a person (section 3). This is the fastest route for conduct and safety
concerns.
(b) **Block** a person who is bothering you. Blocking also tells the Academy (see the Blocking, Suspension and
Enforcement Policy).
(c) **Feedback to the administration** in the app, for comments about a Teacher or about the Service.
(d) **Your family's conversation with the Academy** in the app.
(e) **E-mail**: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for reports and complaints; {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}} for child-safety concerns;
{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} for privacy requests.

2.2 You may use more than one route. A concern raised outside the report tool is recorded by the staff member who
receives it, so that it is handled under this policy.

## 3. Reporting a message or a person in the app

3.1 **Who can report.** Every Parent, Teacher and staff member can report, from their own signed-in account.
A child uses the Parent's device under the Parent's supervision, and the Parent reports on the child's behalf.

3.2 **What you can report.**
(a) **A message** that someone else sent and that you can see now, in a chat you belong to: a text, voice
note, photo, video, file or forwarded message.
(b) **A person** you share, or have shared, a conversation with, from their profile or from the member list of
a group.

3.3 **How to report.** Choose **Report** on the message, or **Report this person** on the person's profile or
member row. Select a reason, add a note if you wish, and choose **Send report**.

3.4 **Reasons.** You choose one of five reasons:
(a) **Harassment or bullying**;
(b) **Inappropriate or sexual content**;
(c) **Spam or scam**;
(d) **A child's safety**; or
(e) **Something else**, for which a short note is required so that we understand the problem.
A note is optional for the other reasons and may be up to 500 characters. Describe what happened; do not copy
the harmful content into the note.

3.5 **What cannot be reported in the app.** You cannot report your own messages, the Academy's lesson cards and
system messages, the Academy's official account, a department manager or the Super Admin. A concern about a
manager or the Super Admin, or about anything the report tool does not cover, can be sent by e-mail to
{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} (or, for a child-safety concern, to {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}). It is handled under this policy by
a person who is not the subject of the concern.

3.6 **One report per subject.** While your report about a message or a person is still open, reporting the same
thing again does not open a second report; the app tells you it is already being reviewed. If you report it
again for a child's safety, the open report becomes an urgent child-safety report.

3.7 **Limit.** You can send up to 10 reports in any hour. This protects the Service from misuse. In an urgent
case, if you reach the limit, write to {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}} or {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

3.8 **Acceptance of the terms.** The app may ask you to accept the terms for your account kind and the Code of
Conduct before you can report. You can always raise a child-safety concern by e-mail at
{{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}.

person's behalf.

## 4. What happens after you report

4.1 **Confirmation.** The app confirms that we received your report.

4.2 **Following your report.** You can follow each of your reports in **My reports**, which shows its status
(**Received**, **Being reviewed**, **Resolved** where we took action, or **Closed** where we took no action) and
our answer.

4.3 **Response time.** We aim to review every report and answer the reporter within **24 hours**; child-safety
reports are escalated immediately (section 8).

4.4 **You always receive an answer.** A report is closed only with a written answer to the reporter, and you are
notified when the answer is ready. To protect the privacy and safety of others, the answer tells you whether we
took action but may not describe the measure in detail. In child-safety cases the answer is a neutral
confirmation that we acted on your report.

4.5 **Your identity is protected.** Only authorised Academy staff see your report (section 10.2). The person you
report is not told that a report exists or who made it. If we remove a message, it looks like any other message
removed by the Academy. If we give a warning, it is a general reminder of the Code of Conduct that never mentions
a report, a reporter or a message. We disclose a reporter's identity only where the law requires it, or to the
competent authorities in a child-safety case.

## 5. Parents reporting a Teacher's or staff member's conduct

5.1 If a Teacher or staff member behaves in a way that breaks the Code of Conduct, report the message or the
person in the app (section 3).

5.2 For concerns about the quality of teaching, punctuality or a lesson, you can also send feedback to the
administration in the app, or write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

5.3 You may ask the Academy to assign your child to a different Teacher. The Academy decides on reassignment and,
where it can, arranges it without interrupting your child's lessons.

5.4 Any concern that a Teacher or staff member may harm a child must be reported with the reason **A child's
safety** or to {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}. Section 8 applies.

## 6. Teachers reporting inappropriate behaviour

6.1 A Teacher can report a message or a person in the app, including a Parent or a staff member, for behaviour
that breaks the Code of Conduct, such as abuse, harassment, inappropriate requests or requests for payment
outside the Academy.

6.2 A Teacher can also block a Parent, or a staff member who is not a manager (see the Blocking, Suspension and
Enforcement Policy). Lessons continue as usual.

6.3 A Teacher who notices a sign that a child may be at risk must report it without delay (Code of Conduct,
section 12), even if the concern is not about something said in the Service.

## 7. Staff reporting

7.1 Staff can report Parents, Teachers and other staff members. Staff cannot block families; they report them.

7.2 A staff member who receives a concern by any route must record it under this policy without delay, and must
not forward, copy or screenshot the content.

## 8. Urgent child-safety concerns

8.1 **In an emergency**, call your local emergency services: {{EMERGENCY_CONTACTS}}.

8.2 **How to report.** In the app, choose the reason **A child's safety**. You can also write to
{{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}. A child-safety concern includes, for example, sexual content involving a child, an
adult seeking sexual or private contact with a child, threats or blackmail, a child saying they are being
harmed, and a request to move a child to private contact outside the Academy.

8.3 **What we do.**
(a) A child-safety report is marked **urgent** as soon as it is sent, and the Super Admin and the staff
specially designated for child safety are notified immediately, at any hour.
(b) **Restricted access.** Only the Super Admin and those designated staff can see or act on a child-safety
report. It never goes to a department queue, and the person reported can never see it.
(c) **Evidence is preserved.** The Academy keeps a snapshot of the reported message's text and a reference to
its attachments; the report record itself is never deleted, even if the message is later deleted. Staff view
the evidence only through the Academy's administration tools, every view is recorded, and the content is never
forwarded, copied or saved elsewhere.

arrange for the child's lessons to continue with another Teacher.
(e) **Authorities.** Where a child is at risk, or where the law requires it, we refer the matter to the
competent authorities and cooperate with them.
(f) **Answer.** You receive a neutral confirmation that we acted on your report.

8.4 The Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy describes this procedure in full.

## 9. How we investigate a report

9.1 **Triage.** Each report goes to the authorised staff of the departments responsible for the person
reported (section 10.2) or, for a child-safety report, to the restricted team in section 8.3(b). A staff member
takes the report and becomes responsible for it.

9.2 **Evidence.** The reviewer looks at the evidence kept with the report: for a message report, a snapshot of
the message's text at the time of the report and a reference to any attachment. Attachments are opened only through
short-lived links issued to the reviewer, and every view is recorded. The reviewer looks only at what is needed
for the decision.

9.3 **Context.** The reviewer may look at the conversation in which the content was sent, the history of
earlier reports and measures concerning the same person, and information from the staff present in the
conversation.

9.4 **Hearing the person reported.** Where it is safe and useful, the Academy may contact the person reported
to hear their account, without revealing the reporter's identity. In a child-safety case we do not do so where
it could put a child at risk or harm an investigation by the authorities.

9.5 **Decision.** The reviewer decides on one of the following outcomes and records the reasons:
(a) **no action**: the report did not show a breach;
(b) **message removed**: the message is deleted for everyone in the conversation;
(c) **warning**: the person receives a conduct warning notice;
(d) **account suspended**: the account is suspended (only staff authorised to administer accounts can do
this, and a reason must be recorded); or
(e) **reassignment**: the Academy's managers are asked to change the arrangement, for example to give the
Student a different Teacher (a reason must be recorded).
The measures and how they escalate are described in the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy.

9.6 **Answer.** The reviewer closes the report with the written answer to the reporter (section 4.4).

## 10. Response times, escalation and confidentiality

10.1 **Escalation clock.** Every report has a 24-hour response time. If it has not been answered after 18 hours,
the responsible managers are alerted. If it has not been answered after 24 hours, the Super Admin is alerted and
the report is marked overdue until it is answered.

10.2 **Who sees a report.** The Super Admin can see every report. Other authorised staff see only the reports
that concern their own departments. Child-safety reports are restricted as described in section 8.3(b). No one
can see or decide a report about themselves.

10.3 **Confidentiality.** Reports, notes and evidence are confidential. They are used only to investigate the
report, protect users, enforce the rules of the Service and meet legal obligations. They are not written into
the Service's technical logs.

## 11. Complaints that are not reports

11.1 A complaint about the Service itself, such as lessons, scheduling, a Teacher's punctuality, billing,
payments, refunds or the app, is not a conduct report. Raise it:
(a) through feedback to the administration, or your family's conversation with the Academy, in the app; or
(b) by e-mail to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

11.2 Privacy requests and complaints (for example, about access to or deletion of personal data) go to
{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and are handled as described in the Privacy Policy.

11.3 Please include your name, the account concerned, the child's name where relevant, and a clear description
of the problem. Never send card numbers or passwords.

11.4 We acknowledge a complaint and aim to give a substantive answer within {{COMPLAINT_RESPONSE_DAYS}} days
[TO CONFIRM]. If we need more time, we tell you why and when to expect an answer.

11.5 If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can ask for the complaint to be reviewed by a manager of the
department concerned, by replying to our answer or by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## 12. Records

12.1 **Reports are never deleted.** Every report, its evidence, its handling and its answer are kept as a record.
The snapshot of the reported message's text is kept even if the reported message is deleted or the person reported deletes their account. If
the reporter deletes their account, the report is kept without the link to the reporter's identity.

12.2 Records are kept for as long as the relationship with the Academy lasts and then as set out in the
retention schedule in the Privacy Policy [TO CONFIRM].

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** The retention period for report records is an open owner
> decision (LD-7). Section 12.2 states it once decided.

12.3 Access to the records is limited as described in section 10.2, and every view of a report's evidence is
recorded.

## 13. Follow-up

13.1 If something happens again after your report is closed, send a new report. Earlier reports about the same
person are taken into account.

13.2 If you have further information about an open report, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and mention that you have
already reported it in the app.

13.3 If you disagree with a measure taken against you, you can appeal as described in the Blocking, Suspension and
Enforcement Policy.

## 14. False or abusive reports

14.1 An honest report that turns out to be mistaken is never held against you.

14.2 Knowingly making a false report, or using reports to harass or retaliate against someone, breaks the Code of
Conduct and may lead to the measures in the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy.

## 15. External complaint routes

15.1 You may also complain to an authority outside the Academy, such as the data protection authority or the
consumer protection authority of your country. Details of the data protection routes are in the Privacy Policy.

15.2 This policy does not limit any right you have under the law of your country.

## 16. Limits of this policy

16.1 The Academy is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee that a report is read the moment it is sent.
In an emergency, always contact your local emergency services first.

16.2 The limits in sections 3.6 and 3.7 apply to reports in the app. They do not limit reports by e-mail.
