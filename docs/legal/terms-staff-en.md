# Staff Terms

**Version** {{VERSION}} · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](terms-staff-ar.md)

## 1. Scope

1.1 These Staff Terms apply to every member of Jawwid Academy's staff who has a staff account in the Service operated by {{ENTITY_NAME}}: supervisors, managers, administrators, the Super Admin, finance, technical, academic and specialist staff, and Sales.

1.2 Your employment or service contract and the Academy's internal policies remain in force. These Staff Terms set the rules for using the Service and are incorporated into your contract by reference. If they conflict, the stricter rule on child safety, confidentiality and data protection applies.

1.3 The Code of Conduct forms part of these Staff Terms. You must also apply the Reporting and Complaints Policy, the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy, the Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy and the Privacy Policy in your work.

1.4 You accept these Staff Terms and the Code of Conduct electronically. We record the version, the time (from our server's clock), the app or portal and its language. You must accept them before you send or upload any Content, and accept a new version after a material change.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** The recording of acceptance (version, server time, client, language) is built in PR #462, which is still a draft and not on `main`. Clause 1.4 must not be published until that change is merged and released.

1.5 Words defined in the Terms of Use have the same meaning here.

## 2. Least-privilege access

2.1 Each staff role and department has only the permissions it needs. Some permissions (for example the child-safety permission) are granted by the Super Admin and cannot be delegated.

2.2 Use only the access your role gives you, and only for your work. Do not try to obtain wider access, and do not use another person's account.

2.3 If you have access you do not need, or see data you should not see, tell your manager so that it can be corrected.

2.4 Keep your password secret and protect your devices with a screen lock. Never share your account. Sign out of the Admin Web on shared computers. Do not choose "Keep me signed in on this device" on a device others use. (The Super Admin's sign-in is never remembered on a device.)

## 3. Scope rules

3.1 Your access is limited to your department and to the families, Students or Teachers you serve. For example:

(a) Operations staff see and act for the families they serve;

(b) Teachers-department staff act for Teachers, within the scope the Academy sets for them;

(c) Sales staff see families within their sales scope;

(d) finance, technical and academic staff never message families; and

(e) only the Super Admin sees all areas.

3.2 A family's contact details and location (country and region) are visible only to the Super Admin, Operations staff for the families they serve, and Sales. Never pass them to a Teacher, to another family or to anyone outside the Academy.

3.3 Contact families only through the Service, and never set up a private channel between a Teacher and a family.

## 4. Support sessions

4.1 A support session lets you see the app as a Parent or Teacher sees it and do a limited set of actions for them. It is a powerful tool and must be used only to help that person.

4.2 You may open a support session only if:

(a) you are one of the people who may do so: staff of the Operations department (for parent accounts, within the families they serve or under a recorded access grant) or the Teachers department (for teacher accounts, within their scope), and the Super Admin. Sales staff cannot. Staff accounts can never be opened this way, and you can never open your own;

(b) the account is within your scope (Teachers-department staff: within the scope the Academy sets for them; the Super Admin: any parent or teacher account);

(c) there is a genuine support reason, such as a request from the person or a problem you must fix for them; and

(d) you write that reason truthfully in the reason field (up to 500 characters).

4.3 Time limits: a session lasts 15 minutes at a time; you may extend it in 15-minute steps, but it always ends no later than 60 minutes after it started. There is no refresh token. You may have only one live session at a time. End the session as soon as the task is done.

4.4 You can see what the person sees, including the person's own list of Reports and blocked people. Permitted actions are limited to: editing the person's own profile and photo, and their children's names, "about" texts and photos; a Teacher's professional profile, certificates and leave requests; uploading files; sending messages and attachments; starring; deleting for themselves; and chat preferences. Everything else is blocked, including calls, lesson reports and bookings, creating conversations, editing or deleting messages for everyone, forwarding, reactions, pins, filing Reports, blocking or unblocking, feedback, accepting terms and acknowledging notices.

4.5 Messages you send in a support session are labelled "Sent by support" to everyone in the conversation. The person is not shown a notice when the session starts, which makes your honesty essential.

4.6 Never open a support session out of curiosity, to read private conversations without a support need, to check on someone, or for any personal reason.

4.7 Every session (you, the account, the reason, the start, expiry and end) and every request in it is recorded permanently and attributed to you. The Super Admin can see every session. Misuse is a disciplinary matter and may lead to the loss of access and to termination of your contract.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** There is no user notice and no second-person approval for support sessions (owner decision LD-3). The review cadence for support sessions (who reviews, how often) is not defined in code and is TO CONFIRM.

## 5. Handling children's data

5.1 Children have no accounts. Their data (name, level, Teacher, photo, about text, age or date of birth, lesson records) is held under their Parent's account. Treat it with special care.

5.2 Open a Child's data only when your work needs it. Never share a Child's photo, name or details outside the Service, including on personal devices or social media.

5.3 Child-safety material must never be copied, screenshotted, downloaded or forwarded.

## 6. Reports, blocks and moderation duties

6.1 Staff can report families and others, but staff cannot block families. Parents and Teachers can block people; the Academy's official account, managers and the Super Admin cannot be blocked. When a Parent or Teacher blocks someone, the responsible manager is told and should check whether help is needed.

6.2 If you have the moderation permission, you must handle Reports for your department:

(a) take each Report and aim to review it and answer the reporter within 24 hours of filing (managers are alerted at 18 hours, the Super Admin at 24 hours); child-safety reports are escalated immediately;

(b) view only what you need to decide it; every view is recorded;

(c) decide an outcome: no action, message removed, Warning, account suspended, or reassignment (for example a different Teacher), following the enforcement ladder in the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy; and

(d) make sure the reporter receives an answer.

6.3 **Child-safety reports** are urgent and restricted. Only the Super Admin and staff holding the child-safety permission can see or act on them, and they are never in department queues. If a child-safety concern reaches you in any other way (a message, a call, a complaint, something you see), enter it as a child-safety Report at once or tell the child-safety point of contact, and keep no copy. Follow the Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy.

6.4 Do not tell the reported person who reported them, and do not discuss a Report with anyone who does not need to know.

## 7. Account actions

7.1 Within your scope you may reset a password, sign an account out, suspend it or reactivate it. Each action needs a reason and is recorded.

7.2 Take enforcement action only in proportion and in line with the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy. When a Child's lessons are paused, the Academy will tell the family that the Child's lessons are paused, in these words: "Lessons for [child's name] are temporarily paused. The academy will contact you." Make sure the family is contacted.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** The message is not yet built; the delivery channel for a suspended family is open (DEV-CONFIRM #27).

7.3 Refunds may be made only by staff authorised to verify payments. Never take or request payments outside the methods the Academy has approved.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Everything you see in the Service about families, Children, Teachers, Staff and the Academy is confidential. Use it only for your work.

8.2 This duty continues after your account is closed or your contract ends.

## 9. No export of personal data

9.1 Do not copy, download, export, photograph, print or send personal data outside the Service, including to personal e-mail, personal devices, messaging apps or AI tools, unless a documented Academy procedure requires it (for example answering a data access request) and your manager has approved it.

9.2 Do not keep personal data after the task that needed it is done.

## 10. Incident reporting

10.1 If you think personal data has been lost, exposed, sent to the wrong person or misused, or that an account has been compromised, report it immediately to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and to your manager. Do not try to hide or fix it alone.

10.2 Report child-safety concerns immediately as set out in clause 6.3, and to {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}.

## 11. Audit and monitoring

11.1 Your actions in the Service are recorded in an audit log, including account actions, record changes, payment actions, Report views and support sessions. The audit log is kept after your account is closed.

11.2 The Academy may review these records to check that access is used properly.

## 12. Account closure

12.1 Staff cannot delete their own accounts. The Super Admin or your department manager closes your staff account when you leave the Academy or no longer need it. Closure for offboarding is permanent.

12.2 After closure, the records you created and the audit log are kept. The Privacy Policy explains how staff data is handled.

## 13. Consequences of breach

13.1 A breach of these Staff Terms or the Code of Conduct may lead to the removal of access, suspension of your staff account, disciplinary action under your contract, termination of your contract and, where the law requires, a report to the competent authorities.
