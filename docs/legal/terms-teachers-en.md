# Teacher Terms

**Version** {{VERSION}} · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](terms-teachers-ar.md)

## 1. Scope and your contract

1.1 These Teacher Terms apply to every Teacher who uses the Service operated by {{ENTITY_NAME}} for Jawwid Academy ("Jawwid", "the Academy", "we").

1.2 Your employment or service contract with the Academy remains the main agreement about your work, pay and duties. These Teacher Terms do not replace it. They set the rules for using the Service and are incorporated into that contract by reference. If the two conflict, your contract applies, except that the safeguarding and data protection rules in sections 5, 6 and 8 always apply as a minimum.

1.3 The Code of Conduct forms part of these Teacher Terms. The Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy, the Reporting and Complaints Policy and the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy also apply to you. The Privacy Policy explains how the Academy handles your personal data.

1.4 You accept these Teacher Terms and the Code of Conduct electronically. We record the version, the time (from our server's clock), the app or portal and its language. You must accept them before you send or upload any Content. For a material change we will ask you to accept the new version.

Clause 1.4 must not be published until that change is merged and released. Whether the in-app child-safety report stays available before acceptance is owner decision LD-11; clause 1.4 must follow it.

1.5 Words defined in the Terms of Use (such as Service, Parent, Child, Student, Staff, Family group, Student group and Content) have the same meaning here.

## 2. Your account and its security

2.1 The Academy creates your teacher account. You must keep your details accurate.

2.2 Keep your password secret and do not share your account with anyone. Academy Staff will never ask for your password.

2.3 Use a device protected by a screen lock, and turn on the app lock where it is available. Sign out of the teacher web portal on shared computers, and do not choose "Keep me signed in on this device" on a device others use.

2.4 Tell the Academy immediately through the app, or at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}, if you think someone else has used your account or if a device with access to it is lost.

## 3. Using the teacher app and the teacher web portal

3.1 You may use the Service only to teach the Students assigned to you and to communicate with the Academy about your work.

3.2 The Service shows you only what you need to teach: your own Students' student profiles (for example name, level, photo and lesson records), your schedule, lesson materials and your own records. You never see a Parent's phone number, e-mail address, country or region, or a family's payments. You must not try to obtain them.

3.3 Lessons and calls are audio only and are not recorded by the Academy. You must never record them (clause 5.5).

3.4 You may move a single lesson only within the notice and limits the app sets. A change to a Student's regular weekly time is a request that the Teachers department decides.

3.5 If you cannot teach a lesson, send a teacher apology in the app before the lesson starts, so that a make-up can be arranged. Do not cancel lessons in any other way.

## 4. Professional conduct

4.1 You must:

(a) teach with care, patience and respect for every Child and family;

(b) be on time for every lesson and teach the full lesson;

(c) use language and Content suitable for children;

(d) follow the Academy's curriculum, lesson materials and academic guidance;

(e) follow the Code of Conduct and your supervisors' reasonable instructions; and

(f) treat Staff and other Teachers with respect.

4.2 You must not use the Service to promote yourself, other services, or any political or commercial cause.

## 5. Safeguarding duties

5.1 The safety of children comes first. These duties apply at all times, whatever your contract says.

5.2 **Mandatory reporting.** If you have any concern about a Child's safety, including anything a Child tells you, any sign of harm, or any inappropriate behaviour by anyone, you must report it without delay:

(a) through the app's child-safety report (choose the reason "A child's safety"); and

(b) by e-mail to {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}.

If a Child appears to be in immediate danger, also tell the Academy straight away by the fastest means available. Do not investigate yourself, do not confront the person concerned, and do not keep or forward copies, screenshots or recordings of the material.

5.3 **No private contact.** You must not contact a Child or a family outside the Service, by any means (phone, messaging apps, social media, e-mail or in person), and must not invite them to contact you. All communication takes place in the Family or Student groups, where Academy Staff are present. If a family or Child tries to contact you privately, do not reply privately; tell your supervisor.

5.4 **No collecting contact details.** You must not ask for, collect, keep or use a family's or Child's phone number, e-mail address, address, location or social media accounts.

5.5 **No recording or images.** No one may record, photograph, screenshot or redistribute a lesson or call without the Academy's prior written permission. Teachers and staff may never record a lesson or call. Lessons are not recorded by the Academy. You must not take photos of Children, or copy, keep or share any image, voice note or video of a Child, inside or outside the Service, except as the Service itself displays it to you.

5.6 **No off-platform payments.** You must not ask for or accept any payment, gift of value or private lesson arrangement from a family. All payments go to the Academy.

5.7 **Appropriate relationship.** You must keep a professional relationship with every Child and family. Do not share personal matters or Content unrelated to the lesson, and never ask a Child to keep a secret from their parents.

## 6. Confidentiality and data protection

6.1 Information you see in the Service about Children, families, Staff and the Academy is confidential. Use it only to teach your assigned Students, and only while you work with the Academy.

6.2 You must not copy, download, export, photograph or share personal data outside the Service, and must not keep it after your work with the Academy ends.

6.3 If you think personal data has been lost, exposed or misused, tell the Academy immediately at {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

6.4 These duties continue after your account is closed or your contract ends.

## 7. Lessons, reports and records

7.1 After every lesson you must record attendance and write an accurate, honest lesson report (what was covered, and any homework, rating or note).

7.2 Record an absence only if the Student truly did not attend. You are never the judge of a family's late apology; a supervisor decides it.

7.3 Corrections to outcomes and reports are made only through the recorded change process in the Service. Never alter a record to hide a mistake.

7.4 Lesson reports, attendance and other records you create are the Academy's educational records and are kept by the Academy.

## 8. Content and intellectual property

8.1 The Academy's curriculum, lesson materials, lesson audio, whiteboard templates and other materials belong to the Academy or its licensors. Quran texts and recitations and other third-party materials are used with permission or under the applicable licence. You may use them only to teach through the Service, and must not copy, sell or use them elsewhere.

8.2 You keep the rights you have in your own original Content (for example your messages, whiteboard writing or teaching notes). You give the Academy a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to store, copy, display and use that Content as needed to provide the Service, to keep educational records, to keep the Service safe and to comply with the law. Your employment or service contract may give the Academy further rights.

8.3 You must not upload Content that infringes someone else's rights.

## 9. Support sessions by the Teachers department

9.1 To help you, an authorised staff member of the Teachers department may open a support session on your account. In it, the staff member sees the app as you see it, including your own list of reports and blocked people, and can do a limited set of things for you.

9.2 Who: only active Teachers-department staff, within the scope the Academy sets for them, and the Super Admin. Sales staff cannot. Staff accounts can never be opened this way, and nobody can open a support session on their own account.

9.3 How: a written reason is required each time. A session lasts 15 minutes at a time; the staff member may extend it in 15-minute steps, but it always ends no later than 60 minutes after it started. There is no refresh token. A staff member can have only one live session at a time.

9.4 What they can do: edit your own profile and photo, your professional profile and certificates, and your leave requests; upload files; send messages and attachments; star messages; delete messages for themselves; and change chat preferences.

9.5 What they cannot do: make or answer calls; write or change lesson reports; book or change lessons; create conversations; edit messages or delete them for everyone; forward; react or pin; file Reports, block or unblock; send feedback; accept terms; or acknowledge notices.

9.6 Messages sent in a support session are labelled "Sent by support" for everyone in the conversation, without naming the staff member. You are not shown a notice when a session starts, and the session does not appear in your devices list.

9.7 Every session (staff member, account, reason, start, expiry and end) and every request in it is recorded permanently. If the record cannot be written, the action fails.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** There is no start notice to the user and no second-person approval for support sessions (owner decision LD-3). Clause 9.6 states the current behaviour and must be updated if it changes.

## 10. Monitoring and audit records

10.1 Academy Staff are present in the Family and Student groups and may read all messages in them, for supervision, quality and safety.

10.2 The Academy keeps supervision records about your work, such as lateness, absences, apologies, leave, complaints, rewards and decisions taken.

10.3 Actions in the Service are recorded in an audit log, including changes to records, support sessions, and views of whiteboards. Lessons and calls are not recorded.

10.4 Reports about you are reviewed under the Reporting and Complaints Policy.

## 11. Reports and blocking

11.1 You can report a message or a person in the app, and you can block a person you are in a conversation with.

11.2 A Block never interrupts lessons: lesson calls still ring, lesson cards and system messages still appear, and the Academy's administrative channel stays open. The Academy's official account, managers and the Super Admin cannot be blocked. When you block someone, the manager of your supervisor's department is told (or the Super Admin if there is none).

11.3 You must not misuse Reports or Blocks.

## 12. Deleting your account

12.1 You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account, with your password) or through the public web page the Academy provides for this.

12.2 If you still have future lessons, assigned Students, or open payroll for this month or last month, you request deletion in the same places. The app shows the date by which your account will be deleted (the web page shows it after you submit). The Academy then hands over your Students and settles your pay, and your account is deleted within 30 days of the request at the latest, even if something is still open. Until then your account works as usual, and you can cancel the request in the app.

12.3 When deletion goes ahead, it takes effect immediately. Your sign-in, password, devices, notifications, phone number and e-mail, professional profile, certificate files and payout account details (the payout account details at the time set out in the Privacy Policy, section 10.4.1) are deleted, and your name is replaced with "Deleted user". Your Teachers-department manager is told.

12.4 The Academy keeps the messages you sent (shown as "Deleted user"), lesson reports and other educational records, pay and disciplinary records, and the audit log. The Privacy Policy explains this in full.

12.5 Deleting your account does not end your employment or service contract. Ending that contract is governed by the contract.

## 13. Suspension and termination

13.1 If you break these Teacher Terms or the Code of Conduct, the Academy may take proportionate steps, normally: removing Content; a Warning; a temporary restriction (for example reassigning your Students or limiting features); a temporary Suspension of your account (all your sessions are signed out); or Termination (your account is closed indefinitely and the Academy will not reactivate it).

13.2 Child-safety concerns, threats, sexual content involving minors or grooming may lead straight to Suspension or Termination and to a report to the competent authorities. The Academy may restrict your account urgently while it investigates.

13.3 A breach of these Teacher Terms may also be a disciplinary matter under your contract.

13.4 Every enforcement measure (removal of content, a warning, a restriction, a suspension or a closure) can be appealed by e-mail to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 14 days [TO CONFIRM]. A staff member not involved in the original decision will review it. This does not affect any procedure in your contract.
