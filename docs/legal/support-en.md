# Help and Support

**Version** 1.0 · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](support-ar.md)

This page explains how to reach Jawwid Academy ("Jawwid", "the Academy", "we") for help with the Jawwid app, the teacher web portal, your lessons, payments, your account and your personal data. The Academy is operated by Jawwid LLC.

## 1. If a child is in danger

1.1 If a child is in immediate danger, contact the emergency number in your country first. Then tell the Academy.

1.2 For any child-safety concern, write to info@jawwid.com, or file a report in the app with the reason "A child's safety". Child-safety messages are read every day. The [Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy](child-safety-policy-en.md) explains what happens next.

## 2. How to contact us

2.1 **In the app.** Families can write to the Academy in their conversation with the Academy, or use the feedback option. This is the quickest way to reach us about lessons and schedules.

2.2 **By e-mail.** One address for everything: info@jawwid.com. Use it for general help, payments, complaints, questions about your personal data and requests to access, correct or delete it, and child-safety concerns (section 1).

2.3 Please write from the e-mail address you use to sign in, and do not send passwords or card details. We will never ask you for your password.

## 3. Common questions

3.1 **I cannot sign in.** Parents can reset their password, or find their sign-in e-mail, from the sign-in screen. If that does not work, or if you are a teacher, write to info@jawwid.com from the e-mail address on your account.

3.2 **I want to delete my account.** Parents and teachers can delete their account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on the web without the app. The Account Deletion page explains both ways, and what is deleted and what is kept: [Account Deletion](account-deletion-en.md).

3.3 **I want to report a person or a message.** Use the report option in the app. Section 12 of the Terms of Use explains how reports are handled.

3.4 **A question about a payment or a refund.** Write in your conversation with the Academy or to info@jawwid.com. Refunds are handled under section 14 of the Terms of Use.

## 4. Complaints

4.1 We acknowledge a complaint and aim to give a substantive answer within 30 days. If you disagree with a decision we made about your account or your content, you can appeal under section 17 of the Terms of Use.
