# Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy

**Version** {{VERSION}} · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](child-safety-policy-ar.md)

## 1. Scope and commitment

1.1 Jawwid Academy ("Jawwid", the "Academy", "we") is operated by {{ENTITY_NAME}}, registered in {{ENTITY_COUNTRY}} under number {{ENTITY_REG_NO}}, at {{ENTITY_ADDRESS}}. The Academy teaches children the Quran (memorisation and recitation), Arabic and Islamic studies in live one-to-one online lessons.

1.2 This Policy applies to the Service: the Jawwid apps for Android and iOS, the teacher web portal, the Admin Web and the lessons. It binds every Parent, Teacher and member of Academy Staff, including supervisors, managers, administrators and the Super Admin.

1.3 This Policy is also the Academy's **published child safety standards**. The safety of children comes before every other consideration in the Service, including lesson continuity, commercial interests and the convenience of adults.

1.4 **Zero tolerance.** The Academy prohibits, and does not tolerate in any form:
(a) child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), meaning any conduct that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers a child;
(b) child sexual abuse material (CSAM), meaning any image, video, audio, drawing, text or other content that depicts or describes a child in a sexual manner, whether real, edited or generated, and any request for, creation, possession, sending or sharing of such content;
(c) grooming, meaning any attempt by an adult to build trust or a relationship with a child, or with the child's family, in order to gain private, sexual or otherwise inappropriate contact with the child;
(d) sextortion, meaning threatening a child or a family in order to obtain sexual content, money, contact or anything else;
(e) any sexual comment, sexual content or sexual contact-seeking directed at or involving a child; and
(f) any attempt to move a child to private contact outside the Academy's channels.

1.5 When the Academy becomes aware of CSAM or of CSAE, it removes the content from the Service for users, preserves the evidence as the law requires, suspends the accounts involved and then closes them indefinitely (the Academy will not reactivate them or accept a new enrolment from the same person), and reports the matter to the competent authorities as set out in section 9 and section 10.

1.6 This Policy works together with the Terms of Use, the Teacher Terms, the Staff Terms, the Code of Conduct, the Reporting and Complaints Policy, the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy and the Privacy Policy. Where any of them is less protective of a child than this Policy, this Policy applies.

1.7 Child-safety point of contact: {{SAFEGUARDING_CONTACT_NAME}}, {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}. This person speaks for the Academy on its child-safety practice and can answer questions about these standards.

## 2. Ages served and who holds the account

2.1 The Academy's lessons are designed primarily for children aged 5 to 12. A child may be enrolled from the age of 3. Older students may also be enrolled; in this Policy, "Child" means any Student under 18 years of age, and every protection in this Policy applies to every Child.

2.2 Children do not have accounts and do not sign in. Every family account is held by an adult Parent: a parent or legal guardian aged at least {{MIN_ACCOUNT_HOLDER_AGE}}. The Parent is the account holder for all of the family's children and is responsible for everything done under the family account.

2.3 Teacher and Staff accounts are held only by adults engaged by the Academy.

## 3. Parent or guardian consent

3.1 The family account is created by the Academy at the Parent's request, and each child is enrolled only at the Parent's request. By enrolling a child, the Parent:
(a) confirms that they are the child's parent or legal guardian, or otherwise have the legal authority to enrol the child and to consent on the child's behalf;
(b) consents, on the child's behalf, to the child taking part in lessons and to the Academy processing the child's information as described in this Policy and in the Privacy Policy; and
(c) confirms that the information given about the child is accurate.

3.2 Where another adult shares responsibility for the child, the Parent who enrols the child is responsible for making sure that enrolment is not against the wishes or the rights of that adult.

3.3 The Academy keeps a record of each child's enrolment made at the Parent's request. The Parent may withdraw consent at any time, as set out in clause 3.4.

3.4 The Parent may withdraw consent at any time, by asking the Academy to end the child's enrolment ({{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or the family's conversation with the Academy) or by deleting the family account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on the Academy's account-deletion web page. Withdrawing consent stops future lessons and future processing for teaching purposes.

3.5 Withdrawing consent does not delete every record. At present, the child's records kept by the Academy (the child's profile, lesson records, lesson reports and rewards) are kept after the family account is deleted, and messages already sent remain in the conversations, shown as from "Deleted user". A Parent may ask for a copy of their child's information, for its correction or deletion, or for processing to stop, by writing to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. The Privacy Policy explains what is kept, why, and how such requests are handled.

3.6 Consent to a certificate Story is separate and is described in clause 7.4.

## 4. Children's use of the Service

4.1 A child has no account, no login, no password and no contact details of their own in the Service.

4.2 A child uses the Service only on the Parent's device, signed in to the Parent's account, and under the Parent's supervision. Anything typed, sent or said through the family account is treated as coming from the Parent's account.

4.3 The app offers an **app lock** (fingerprint, face recognition or the device passcode or screen lock, where the device supports them), with an automatic lock setting. The Academy recommends that Parents turn it on so that a child cannot open the app, or read or send messages, without the Parent.

4.4 By default, push notifications do not show the names of senders or callers on the lock screen. A Parent may choose to show names; the Academy recommends leaving them hidden on a device a child uses.

4.5 The Parent controls the family's profile photos. A child's profile photo is optional and is added, changed or removed only by the Parent (or by authorised Staff at the Parent's request in a support session, section 13).

## 5. Appropriate communication between teachers, staff and students

5.1 **Lessons.** Lessons are live, one-to-one **audio** calls in the app. There is no video. The Academy does not record lessons or calls. The Academy recommends that a Parent or another responsible adult stays nearby during every lesson.

5.2 **No private channel.** A Teacher and a Parent never have a private, direct conversation in the Service. They communicate only in the Student group or the Family group, where Academy Staff are present. The Service enforces this rule. Parents cannot message other Parents directly.

5.3 **Contact only through the Service.** A Teacher must never contact a child or the child's family outside the Service: no social media, no messaging apps, no phone calls or text messages, no e-mail and no meeting in person. A Teacher must refuse any such contact proposed by a family and tell the Academy. Parents must not invite Teachers to such contact.

5.4 **No contact details or location.** Teachers never receive a Parent's phone number, e-mail address, country, region or location, nor the family's payments. A Teacher sees only the student profile of the Teacher's own students. Teachers must not ask a child or a Parent for contact details, an address, a school or any other way to reach them.

5.5 **No gifts or private payments.** Teachers and Staff must not give or accept gifts, money or favours to or from a child or a family, and must not accept any payment outside the Academy's own payment arrangements.

5.6 **Limited staff contact.** Finance, technical and academic staff never message families. Families are contacted only by the staff whose work requires it, such as the family's supervisor or the Operations team.

5.7 **Conduct towards children.** Every Teacher and member of Staff must:
(a) keep all communication with or about a child professional, kind, age-appropriate and about learning;
(b) never ask a child to keep a secret from their parents or from the Academy;
(c) never ask a child for a photo, a video, a voice recording or personal information;
(d) never make comments about a child's body or appearance, and never use sexual, violent, humiliating or frightening language;
(e) never single out a child for special attention, private praise or a "special relationship" outside the lesson; and
(f) end the lesson and tell the Academy at once if anything during a lesson raises a concern for the child's safety.

## 6. Privacy and protection of children's information

6.1 **Minimum data.** The Academy collects only what it needs to teach the child: the child's name, level, assigned teacher, optional photo, optional "about" text, date of birth or stated age, and lesson records (attendance, lesson reports, corrections, memorisation marks, practice and rewards).

6.2 **Sensitive information.** A child's enrolment in Quran or Islamic-studies lessons, and the related lesson records, may reveal the family's religious belief. The Academy treats this information as sensitive: it is used only to teach the child, seen only by those who need it for that purpose, and never used for any other purpose.

6.3 **Who can see a child's information.**
(a) The Parent sees their own children's information.
(b) The child's Teacher sees the student profile and lesson records of the Teacher's own students only.
(c) Academy Staff see what their role requires, for the families they serve; the Super Admin can see all records.
(d) Parents of other families never see a family's children, except a certificate Story shared with the Parent's consent (clause 7.4).
(e) Our card-payment provider receives the child's name as part of the description of a lesson-package payment. The Privacy Policy lists every service provider and what each receives.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** Whether the child's name should be sent to the card-payment provider is an open owner decision (owner decision LD-8). If the name is removed, delete clause 6.3(e).

6.4 **No advertising, analytics or tracking; protective defaults.** The Service shows no advertising (targeted or otherwise), uses no analytics or advertising identifiers, and does not track children or families across other apps or websites. Privacy-protective settings, such as hidden names on the lock screen (clause 4.4), are the default. Error and crash reports are sent to an error-diagnostics service without names, e-mail addresses or message contents.

6.5 **No location.** The Service never uses GPS or device location. A Parent may enter, by hand, the family's country and region (state, emirate, governorate or province). Teachers and Parents of other families never see it, and it is not shared with any third party.

6.6 The Privacy Policy describes in full what information is collected, why, who receives it, where it is stored, how long it is kept and how to exercise privacy rights. Privacy requests may be sent to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

## 7. Children's images, recordings and personal details

7.1 **No recording.** No one may record, photograph, screenshot or redistribute a lesson or call without the Academy's prior written permission. Teachers and staff may never record a lesson or call. Lessons are not recorded by the Academy. No one may take screenshots or photographs of a whiteboard or a group conversation that includes a child in order to share them outside the Service.

7.2 **Children's photos.** A child's profile photo is optional and is chosen by the Parent. Other families never see a child's photo or information, except in a certificate Story that the Parent agreed to publish (clause 7.4). Teachers and Staff must never take, ask for, keep or share a photo, video or recording of a child, other than what the Service itself shows them and a certificate Story published with the Parent's agreement. Parents should send images or videos of a child in a conversation only when it is needed for learning, and never an image that shows the child undressed, in a private setting, or with details that identify where the child lives or studies.

7.3 **Personal details.** No one may post in a group, or anywhere in the Service, a child's full name together with the family name, school, home address, phone number, location or other details that would allow the child to be identified or found.

7.4 **Certificates in Stories.** The Academy may publish a child's certificate as an announcement (Story) only after a Parent of the child's family has given consent, and only while that consent stands. The consent, who gave it and how, is recorded. A Parent may withdraw it at any time by telling the Academy; once consent is withdrawn, or if none was given, no Story is published. A Story's media is deleted 72 hours after the Story ends.

## 9. Safeguarding procedure

9.1 **Escalation at once.** Every child-safety report, and every child-safety concern that reaches the Academy in any other way, is treated as urgent. It is escalated immediately to the child-safety point of contact, the Super Admin and the staff specially authorised to handle child-safety reports. It never goes into an ordinary department queue.

9.2 **Restricted access.** Only the Super Admin and the staff individually authorised for child-safety cases can see or act on a child-safety report. The reported person never sees it. Every view of a report and of its evidence is recorded.

9.3 **Immediate danger first.** If a child appears to be in immediate danger, the Academy first contacts the emergency services of the child's country, or asks the Parent to do so, and then continues this procedure.

9.4 **Evidence.** The Academy keeps a snapshot of the reported message's text and a reference to its attachments, together with the record of who did what; the report record itself is never deleted. Content is removed from view for users at once. Staff must never forward, copy, download, screenshot or save suspected abusive content, or send it to anyone, and view it only to the extent needed to decide what to do.

9.5 **Protecting the child.** Where a report is credible, the Academy restricts or suspends the account of the person concerned at once, pending investigation. A Teacher who is the subject of a child-safety report gives no lessons while it is investigated, and the child's lessons are reassigned to another Teacher. The family's conversation with the Academy stays open throughout.

9.6 **Informing parents.** The Academy informs the child's Parent, unless doing so could put the child at risk or harm an investigation, in which case the Academy acts on the advice of the competent authorities. The Academy never tells the reported person who made the report. It tells them about a referral to the authorities only if the authorities allow it or legal advice permits it.

9.7 **Referral to the authorities.** Where the law requires it, or where the Academy considers it necessary to protect a child, the Academy reports suspected CSAM, grooming, sextortion or abuse to the competent authorities, through the channel the authority sets, and co-operates with them. Suspected CSAM is never sent by e-mail or chat, including to the authorities, except through the authority's own channel.

9.8 **Outcome.** Accounts found to have been involved in CSAE or CSAM are closed indefinitely, and the Academy will not reactivate them or accept a new enrolment from the same person. Other outcomes follow the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy, which also explains how a decision may be appealed.

9.9 **Answering the reporter.** The person who reported is always answered in the app. To protect the child and any investigation, the answer does not give details of the action taken.

## 10. How to report suspected abuse or exploitation

10.1 **Emergencies.** If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first: {{EMERGENCY_CONTACTS}}. Then tell the Academy.

10.2 **In the app.** Press and hold a message, or open a person's profile, choose **Report**, and select the reason **"A child's safety"**. You may add a note. Child-safety reports go straight to the people named in clause 9.1. You can also block the person (section 11).

10.3 **By e-mail.** Write to the child-safety point of contact at {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}. Please describe what happened, when, and who was involved. **Do not attach, forward or copy any image or video that may show a child being abused**; tell us where it is in the Service so that we can act on it.

10.4 **External reporting lines.** You may also report directly to the police or to the child-protection authorities in your country: {{CHILD_PROTECTION_REPORTING_LINES}}. Reports of child sexual abuse material can also be made to a recognised national hotline, such as a member of the INHOPE network or, for the United States, the NCMEC CyberTipline.

10.5 A child-safety concern can always be reported by e-mail, whether or not the person reporting has an account or has accepted any terms.

10.6 A report made in good faith is never held against the person who made it, even if no concern is found. Deliberately false reports are a breach of the Code of Conduct.

## 11. Reporting and blocking tools in the app

11.1 Every user can report a message or a person. Every report is acknowledged in the app. We aim to review every report and answer the reporter within 24 hours; child-safety reports are escalated immediately (clause 9.1). The record of a report is never deleted. The Reporting and Complaints Policy describes the process.

11.2 A Parent or a Teacher can block another user. The blocked person's messages, notifications and calls no longer reach the person who blocked them. So that lessons are not disrupted, lesson calls, lesson cards and the Academy's administrative messages continue, and the Academy's official account, managers and the Super Admin cannot be blocked. Every block is notified to the responsible manager, so that the Academy can check whether a child needs protection.

## 12. Responsibilities

12.1 **Parents.** Each Parent must:
(a) supervise their child's use of the Service and keep the device, password and app lock secure;
(b) give accurate information about themselves and their children, and keep it up to date;
(c) never share the family account, the password, or any lesson or call with anyone outside the family;
(d) never record or share lessons without the Academy's prior written permission, and follow section 7;
(e) not try to contact a Teacher outside the Service, or give a Teacher personal contact details; and
(f) report any concern about a child's safety at once, as set out in section 10.

12.2 **Teachers and Staff.** Each Teacher and member of Staff must:
(a) follow section 5 and section 7 at all times;
(b) report every child-safety concern immediately, through the in-app report or directly to the child-safety point of contact, even if unsure (and, where the law of their country places a duty to report on those entrusted with a child's education, also as that law requires), and never investigate alone or keep a copy of any content;
(c) keep all information about children and families confidential, use it only for their work, and share it only with those who need it to protect the child; and
(d) co-operate fully with the Academy and with the competent authorities.

12.3 A breach of this Policy by a Teacher or member of Staff is serious misconduct and may lead to immediate suspension, termination and referral to the authorities.

## 13. Support sessions and children's information

13.1 To help a Parent or a Teacher, authorised Academy Staff may open a support session, in which they see the app as that person sees it, including the family's children's information, and can do a limited set of actions for them (for example, edit the person's own profile and photo, and their children's names, "about" texts and photos, or send a message on the person's behalf).

13.2 A support session:
(a) is available only to active staff of the Operations department (for parent accounts, within the families they serve or under a recorded access grant) or the Teachers department (for teacher accounts, within their scope), and the Super Admin. Sales staff cannot. Staff accounts can never be opened this way;
(b) requires a written reason. A session lasts 15 minutes at a time; the staff member may extend it in 15-minute steps, but it always ends no later than 60 minutes after it started. There is no refresh token;
(c) can see what the person sees, including the person's own list of reports and blocked people, but cannot make or join calls, edit lesson reports or bookings, file reports, block or unblock, or accept terms on the person's behalf; and
(d) is recorded permanently: who opened it, for which account, why, and every request made (never the contents).

13.3 Messages sent in a support session are labelled "Sent by support" to everyone in the conversation. At present the person is not shown a notice when a session starts.
