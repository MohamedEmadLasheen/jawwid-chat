# Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy

**Version** {{VERSION}} · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](blocking-and-enforcement-policy-ar.md)

## 1. About this policy

1.1 This policy explains how users of Jawwid Academy ("Jawwid", the "Academy", "we"), operated by
{{ENTITY_NAME}}, can block another person, and the measures the Academy takes when the Code of Conduct, the Terms
of Use, the Teacher Terms or the Staff Terms are broken.

1.2 It forms part of the rules of the Service together with the Code of Conduct, the Reporting and Complaints
Policy, the Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy and the Privacy Policy. Words defined in the Code of
Conduct have the same meaning here.

## 2. Principles

2.1 **Children first.** The safety of children comes before every other consideration.

2.2 **Lessons continue.** Blocking and enforcement are designed not to interrupt a child's education where that
can be avoided safely. Where lessons must pause, the family is told.

2.3 **Proportionality.** A measure matches the seriousness of the breach, its effect on others and any earlier
breaches.

2.4 **Fairness.** Decisions are taken by authorised staff on the evidence, with reasons recorded, and can be
appealed (section 13).

2.5 **Confidentiality.** A measure never reveals who reported the person.

## 3. Blocking another user

3.1 **Who can block whom.**
(a) A **Parent** can block a Teacher, or a staff member, with whom they share a conversation.
(b) A **Teacher** can block a Parent, or a staff member, with whom they share a conversation.
(c) **Staff cannot block** Parents, Teachers or other staff. Staff who are concerned about a user report them
(Reporting and Complaints Policy) and use the Academy's supervision tools.

3.2 **Who cannot be blocked.** The Academy's official account, department managers and the Super Admin cannot be
blocked. They form the Academy's administrative channel, which every family must be able to reach. You can
report them by e-mail instead, as described in the Reporting and Complaints Policy. You also cannot block
yourself, or someone with whom you share no conversation.

3.3 **How to block.** Choose **Block this person** on the person's profile, and confirm. You can see everyone you
blocked in **Blocked people**.

3.4 **What blocking does.** Blocking is enforced by the Academy's servers, not only hidden in the app. After you
block someone:
(a) **Messages.** You no longer see their messages in any conversation you share. Each one is replaced, for you
only, by the line "Message hidden: you blocked this person". The messages are hidden, not deleted, and the
other members of a shared group still see them. Hidden messages are not counted as unread.
(b) **Notifications.** You receive no push notification, mention or Notification Center item caused by their
messages, and any such notification already queued is cancelled.
(c) **Calls.** Their calls do not ring for you. To the caller, a call to you is unavailable, as if the line were
busy.
(d) **Direct conversations.** They cannot open, or reopen, a direct conversation with you.
(e) **Groups.** Nobody leaves a group because of a block.

3.5 **Educational exceptions.** So that a child's lessons are never interrupted, these are never hidden or
refused by a block:
(a) **Lesson calls.** A Teacher's call to a family for a scheduled lesson of a child in that family still rings,
from 15 minutes before the lesson starts until 15 minutes after it ends, even if the Parent has blocked the
Teacher.
(b) **Lesson cards and system messages**, including schedules and cancellations, are always shown.
(c) **Lesson, payment, task and system notifications**, including lesson reminders and schedule changes, are
always delivered.
(d) **The administrative channel.** The Academy's official account, managers and the Super Admin cannot be
blocked, and the family's conversation with the Academy stays open. If the blocked person is the family's
supervisor, messages from the staff member who covers that conversation are delivered.

3.6 **The blocked person.** The blocked person is not sent a notice of the block. They may notice that their
calls are unavailable or that they cannot open a direct conversation with you.

3.7 **The Academy is told.** Every block opens a case for the Academy and notifies each manager responsible for
the supervisors of the family's children (or, for a Teacher, the Teacher's supervising department), once per
case. The notice names the people concerned and gives the manager no access beyond their existing permissions.
If the blocked person is one of those managers, the Super Admin is told instead. The case is reviewed under the
Reporting and Complaints Policy, so that the Academy can follow up, for example by assigning a different Teacher.

3.8 **Unblock.** You can unblock a person at any time from **Blocked people** or their profile. Their messages,
including those sent while they were blocked, are shown again, and they can message and call you again.

3.9 **Limit.** You can block up to 20 people in any 24 hours. Blocking and unblocking the same person again while
the Academy's case is still open does not notify the managers again.

3.10 **Blocking is not a report.** If a person has broken the Code of Conduct, report them as well, so that the
Academy can act. If you no longer want a particular Teacher, ask the Academy to assign a different one.

3.11 **Ending a block.** Blocks end when either person's account is deleted.

3.12 Staff acting in a support session can see the person's list of blocked people, but cannot block or unblock
on the person's behalf.

## 4. The Academy's enforcement measures

4.1 When the Academy finds that its rules have been broken, it may take one or more of the following measures,
from the lightest to the most serious:
(a) removal of content (section 5);
(b) warning (section 6);
(c) temporary restriction (section 7);
(d) temporary suspension (section 8); and
(e) closure of the account (section 9).

4.2 Measures are normally applied in this order, as described in section 10. In severe cases, including any
case involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child, grooming, or threats to a person's life or safety,
the Academy may go straight to suspension or closure of the account and refer the matter to the competent authorities
(section 12).

4.3 Measures are decided by authorised staff: the reviewer of a report, within their department, or the Super
Admin. Only staff authorised to administer accounts can suspend or close an account.

## 5. Removal of content

5.1 Staff who take part in a conversation, and reviewers of a report, can delete a Teacher's or Parent's message
for everyone in the conversation, at any time.

5.2 A removed message looks like any other message removed by the Academy. If the message had been edited, its
earlier versions are kept in the Academy's records.

5.3 When content is removed because of a report, the report keeps the evidence (section 15).

## 6. Warnings

6.1 A warning is a conduct warning notice sent to the person in the app. It is a fixed reminder of the Code of
Conduct and never mentions a report, a reporter or a message. Its text is: "Please keep every conversation in
the academy respectful and appropriate, as the Code of Conduct asks."

6.2 A warning is recorded and counts towards the person's history (section 10).

6.3 The Academy may also explain a breach directly to a person, for example through their supervisor, where this
can be done without revealing a reporter.

## 7. Temporary restrictions

7.1 The Academy may restrict how a person takes part in the Service for a period, while the account stays
active. Examples are:
(a) **reassignment**: assigning a Student to a different Teacher, or a family to a different supervisor;
(b) changing the members of a conversation; and
(c) limiting the use of certain features [TO CONFIRM: which features the Academy can limit].

7.2 Reassignment is arranged by the managers of the departments concerned, so that the child's lessons
continue.

## 8. Temporary suspension

8.1 **What it does.** When an account is suspended, every session of the account on every device is signed out
immediately, its connections are closed, it is removed from any call in progress, its scheduled notifications
are cancelled, and it cannot sign in until the Academy reactivates it.

8.2 **Lessons.** While a family account is suspended, the Academy pauses the children's lessons and will tell
the family that the Child's lessons are paused, in these words: "Lessons for [child's name] are temporarily
paused. The academy will contact you."

8.3 **Teachers.** While a Teacher's account is suspended, the Teacher gives no lessons, and the Academy arranges

8.4 **Reactivation.** A suspension can be lifted by authorised staff. Sessions that were signed out do not come
back; the person signs in again.

8.5 **Reasons.** A reason must be recorded for every suspension decided on a report.

## 9. Closure of the account

9.1 The Academy may close an account, in particular for a severe breach or after repeated breaches.

9.2 When an account is closed, the account is closed indefinitely and the Academy will not reactivate it or
accept a new enrolment from the same person. The person may not use another person's account to return to the
Service.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** No terminal account state or sign-up deny-list exists in code;
> proposal P-6 (outstanding register).

9.3 A Teacher's or staff member's account is also permanently deactivated when their engagement with the Academy
ends.

9.4 What happens to the data of a closed account is described in the Privacy Policy.

## 10. Repeated violations

10.1 Breaches are recorded against the person's account. When the Academy decides on a measure, it takes into
account the earlier reports that led to action, warnings, restrictions and suspensions concerning the same
person.

10.2 A repeated breach normally leads to the next measure on the ladder in section 4.1. A pattern of minor
breaches may lead to suspension or closure of the account.

10.3 A report closed with no action is not counted against the person reported.

10.4 A Teacher's lesson attendance and punctuality are handled under the Teacher Terms and the Academy's
professional procedures, separately from this policy.

## 11. Emergency restrictions pending investigation

11.1 Where the Academy believes that a person, in particular a child, may be at risk, or that serious harm may
continue, it may suspend an account or remove content immediately, before the investigation is complete.

11.2 An emergency restriction is reviewed as soon as the investigation allows. It is lifted if the concern is
not confirmed, and the person may then be told that the restriction has ended.

## 12. Special procedures for child safety

12.1 A concern about a child's safety is handled by the Super Admin and the staff specially designated for child
safety, as described in the Reporting and Complaints Policy and the Child Safety and Parental Responsibility
Policy.

12.2 In such a case the Academy may:
(a) suspend the account concerned immediately (section 11);
(b) remove the content for all users while **preserving the evidence**: the Academy keeps a snapshot of the
reported message's text and a reference to its attachments; the report record itself is never deleted, and the
evidence is never forwarded or copied;
(c) arrange for the child's lessons to continue with another Teacher;
(d) refer the matter to the competent authorities where a child is at risk or the law requires it, and
cooperate with them; and
(e) close the account (section 9).

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** Preserving attachments under a legal hold is planned
> (engineering MG-6/L-ENG-12).

12.3 **No tipping off.** Where telling the person concerned about a measure, its reason or a referral to the
authorities could put a child at risk or harm an investigation, the Academy will not do so, and may delay any
explanation or appeal decision for as long as that risk lasts.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** Section 12.3 requires legal review in each country where the

> protection rights of access.

## 13. Appeals

13.1 Every enforcement measure (removal of content, a warning, a restriction, a suspension or a closure) can be
appealed by e-mail to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 14 days [TO CONFIRM]. The 14 days run from when you learn of the
measure.

13.2 Include your name, the account concerned, the measure and why you believe it was wrong. A suspended person
can appeal by e-mail although they cannot sign in.

13.3 The appeal is reviewed by a staff member who was not involved in the original decision. They may confirm,
reduce or lift the measure.

13.4 We aim to answer an appeal within {{COMPLAINT_RESPONSE_DAYS}} days [TO CONFIRM]. A measure stays in place while the
appeal is reviewed, unless the reviewer decides otherwise.

13.5 Section 12.3 may limit what we can tell you in a child-safety case.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** There is no appeal in the app; appeals are by e-mail only (owner
> decision LD-10). The 14-day period and the response time are to be confirmed by the owner.

## 14. Effect on lessons and payments

14.1 Blocking never pauses lessons (section 3.5).

14.2 A suspension or closure of a family account pauses or ends the children's lessons. A suspension or
closure of a Teacher's account leads the Academy to assign the Teacher's Students to another Teacher where
it can.

14.3 Whether lessons that could not take place because of an enforcement measure are kept in the family's lesson
balance, rescheduled or refunded is decided under the Terms of Use and the Academy's refund rules:
{{REFUND_RULES}}.

14.4 An enforcement measure does not cancel an amount already due to the Academy, except as the refund rules
provide.

14.5 Payments to Teachers are governed by the Teacher Terms.

## 15. Records of enforcement

15.1 Every block, unblock, warning, content removal, suspension, reactivation and report decision is recorded in
the Academy's audit log, with the staff member's identifier, the time and the reason where one is required. The audit log
records identifiers, not message contents.

15.2 Report records, including the snapshot of the reported message's text, are never deleted, and are kept as described in the Reporting and
Complaints Policy.

15.3 Records are kept for as long as the relationship with the Academy lasts and then as set out in the retention
schedule in the Privacy Policy [TO CONFIRM].

15.4 Records are confidential and are used only to enforce the rules of the Service, protect users, handle
appeals and meet legal obligations.
