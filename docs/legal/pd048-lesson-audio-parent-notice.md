# Lesson pronunciation audio: the terms and privacy paragraph (PD-48)

**Approved by the owner on 2026-10-29, word for word** (docs/product/pd048-lesson-voice-over-design.md §17 Q7).

This paragraph belongs in the academy's published terms and privacy notice. The app shows it under Parent Settings → About lesson audio → Terms and privacy. Any terms or privacy page published outside this repository must carry it too, unchanged.

Rules:
- Use the exact words, in both languages. Do not paraphrase or shorten them.
- Do not name the provider. "Our speech provider" stays true whichever vendor is used (release gate R-48a).
- Any change needs the owner's approval. A change of meaning also bumps the notice version in `apps/api/src/lesson-audio/parent-notices.ts`, so every parent is asked again.

## English

> **Lesson pronunciation audio.** Some lessons play the pronunciation of letters, words and sentences. This audio is generated by a computer voice. Before any child hears a recording, two of our teachers listen to it and approve it. A word the computer voice cannot say correctly has no audio. The Quran is never read by a computer voice. The text of our lessons is sent to our speech provider to produce the audio. No information about you or your child is sent.
