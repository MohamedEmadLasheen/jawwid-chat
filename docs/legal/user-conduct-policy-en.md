# Code of Conduct

**Version** {{VERSION}} · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](user-conduct-policy-ar.md)

Jawwid Academy ("Jawwid", the "Academy", "we") is operated by {{ENTITY_NAME}}. We teach children the Quran,
Arabic and Islamic studies in live online lessons. Children learn best, and stay safe, in a place where every
adult behaves with respect, honesty and care. This Code of Conduct sets out the behaviour we expect from
everyone who uses the Service, the content that is not allowed, and what happens when the rules are broken.

We have **zero tolerance** for objectionable content and for abusive users. Any content or behaviour that
breaks this Code may be removed and may lead to the measures described in section 19, up to closure of the
account and referral to the authorities.

## 1. About this Code

1.1 This Code applies to every account kind. Every account accepts it together with the terms for its own
account kind: families accept the Terms of Use, teachers accept the Teacher Terms, and Academy staff accept
the Staff Terms. We record the version you accepted and the time of acceptance. You must accept this Code
before you can send or upload Content.

1.2 This Code works together with:

(b) the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy, which explains blocking and the measures we take;
(c) the Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy, which explains how we protect children; and
(d) the Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal data.

1.3 If this Code and the terms for your account kind deal with the same matter of conduct, the stricter rule
applies.

1.4 In this Code, "Service" means the Jawwid apps (Android and iOS), the teacher web portal, the Admin Web and
the lessons. "Content" means anything a user sends or uploads, including chat messages, images, videos, voice
notes, files, reactions, edits, profile photos, children's photos and feedback to the administration.

## 2. Who this Code applies to

2.1 **Parents.** A parent or legal guardian who holds a family account ("Parent") is bound by this Code and
is responsible for all activity on the family account.

2.2 **Children using a Parent's device.** Children ("Students") do not have accounts of their own. A child
uses the Service on the Parent's device and under the Parent's supervision. Everything sent from a family
account is treated as sent by the Parent, who must make sure that any child or other family member using the
device follows this Code.

2.3 **Teachers.** Every teacher who teaches through the Service ("Teacher") is bound by this Code, in
addition to the Teacher Terms.

2.4 **Academy staff.** Every member of the Academy's staff is bound by this Code, in addition to the Staff
Terms. This includes supervisors, managers, administrators, the Super Admin, and staff in the Operations,
Teachers and Sales departments, whatever their role.

2.5 **Where it applies.** This Code applies to every part of the Service: one-to-one and group chats, audio
calls, live lessons, whiteboards, profiles, feedback to the administration, and the report and block tools.
It also applies to conduct outside the Service where that conduct is directed at another member of the
Academy community because of their participation in the Service, or puts a child of the Academy at risk.

## 3. Respectful communication

3.1 Treat every Parent, Student, Teacher and staff member with courtesy and respect, including when you
disagree.

3.2 Write and speak in a way that is suitable for an educational environment in which children are present.
Assume that a child may see or hear anything you send.

3.3 Raise disagreements about lessons, teachers, schedules or payments calmly, with the Academy, through the
channels described in the Reporting and Complaints Policy. Do not argue in front of a child.

3.4 Do not shout (for example by writing in capitals to intimidate), insult, mock, belittle or humiliate
anyone, including a child who makes a mistake in a lesson.

3.5 Keep each conversation on its purpose: learning, the child's progress, scheduling and the Academy's
administration.

## 4. Harassment, bullying, threats and abuse

4.1 You must not harass, bully, intimidate or abuse anyone. This includes:
(a) repeated unwanted messages or calls after the other person has asked you to stop;
(b) insults, name-calling, ridicule or degrading remarks;
(c) threats of violence or harm against a person, their family or their property, whether serious or
"as a joke";
(d) blackmail, or pressuring anyone with threats of disclosure;
(e) encouraging anyone to harm themselves;
(f) excluding, mocking or targeting a child or adult in a group; and
(g) any form of physical, emotional or verbal abuse of a child, including during a lesson.

4.2 Threats against a person's life or safety, and any abuse of a child, are treated as severe cases under
section 19.3.

## 5. Hate and discrimination

5.1 You must not send Content that attacks, demeans or incites hatred or violence against a person or a group
because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, language, sex, age, disability, social origin or religion.

5.2 You must not treat a Student, Parent, Teacher or staff member unfairly because of any of these
characteristics.

## 6. Objectionable content: what is not allowed

6.1 "Objectionable content" means any Content that falls into one or more of the categories below. You must
not send, upload, share, forward, display or link to objectionable content anywhere in the Service:
(a) **Sexual content** of any kind, including sexually explicit or suggestive text, images, video, audio,
links or jokes, and any sexual content involving a minor (see section 7);
(b) **Nudity** or partial nudity, including images of children in a state of undress;
(c) **Violent or graphic content**, including gore, cruelty to people or animals, and content that glorifies
violence;
(d) **Harassment, bullying and threats**, as described in section 4;
(e) **Hate speech and discrimination**, as described in section 5;
(f) **Self-harm content** that promotes, encourages or instructs in suicide, self-injury or eating disorders;
(g) **Extremist content** that supports, promotes or recruits for terrorism or violent extremism;
(h) **Illegal content and activity**, including the sale or promotion of drugs, weapons or other illegal
goods, and content that is unlawful where it is sent or received;
(i) **Obscene, vulgar or profane language**, including swearing and crude gestures or images;
(j) **Defamatory content**: false statements that damage the reputation of a person or of the Academy;
(k) **Spam, scams and deceptive content**, as described in section 8;
(l) **Malicious content**, including viruses, malware, phishing links and files designed to damage devices or
steal information;
(m) **Content that infringes the rights of others**, including copyright and other intellectual property,
and the redistribution of lessons and materials described in section 13;
(n) **Personal information of others** shared without authorisation, as described in section 9; and
(o) **Any other content that is inappropriate for an educational environment for children**, including
gambling, alcohol, tobacco and smoking, and content that mocks religion (see section 16).

6.2 Whether Content is objectionable is decided by the Academy, taking into account its context, its audience
(in particular whether children may see it) and its effect on others. The Academy's decision is final, subject
to the appeal described in the Blocking, Suspension and Enforcement Policy.

6.3 Messages are not checked automatically before they are sent. They are published immediately, and Academy
staff who take part in a conversation can remove a Teacher's or Parent's message from that conversation at any
time. Every user can report Content (section 20).

## 7. Child sexual abuse and exploitation, and grooming: absolute prohibition

7.1 The Academy has an **absolute prohibition** on child sexual abuse and exploitation. You must never:
(a) create, send, upload, request, store, share or link to any sexual image, video, audio or text involving a
person under 18, whether real, drawn or computer-generated;
(b) engage in sexual or sexualised conversation with a child, or with a Parent about a child;
(c) **groom** a child. Grooming means building a relationship, trust or emotional connection with a child in
order to manipulate, exploit or abuse them. Warning signs include: seeking private contact with a child;
giving a child gifts, money or special attention in secret; asking a child to keep secrets from their
parents or the Academy; asking for photos of a child; and moving the conversation to another app or to an
in-person meeting;
(d) attempt to obtain a child's personal contact details, social-media accounts, location or images; or
(e) threaten, blackmail or extort a child or a family, including with images (sextortion).

7.2 Any breach of section 7.1 is a severe case. The Academy may suspend the account immediately, will
preserve the evidence, and will refer the matter to the competent authorities where the law requires it or a
child is at risk. Details are in the Child Safety and Parental Responsibility Policy.

## 8. Spam, scams and payments outside the Academy

8.1 You must not send spam: unsolicited advertising, chain messages, repeated identical messages, or messages
unrelated to the Academy's lessons and administration.

8.2 You must not attempt to deceive or defraud anyone, including through fake offers, false claims about
payments, requests for money, or requests for passwords, one-time codes or bank details.

8.3 **No payments outside the Academy.** Payments for lessons are made only to the Academy, through the
payment methods the Academy offers. You must not:
(a) as a Teacher or staff member, offer, request or accept private lessons, payment, gifts of money or any
other benefit from a family outside the Academy;
(b) as a Parent, offer a Teacher or staff member private lessons or payment outside the Academy; or
(c) ask anyone to pay into a personal account.

8.4 If you receive a request to pay someone outside the Academy, report it (section 20).

## 9. Personal information of others

9.1 You must not collect, request, share or publish another person's personal information without their
authorisation and a legitimate reason connected to the Service. This includes:
(a) phone numbers, e-mail addresses and home addresses;
(b) location information;
(c) photos, videos or voice notes of another person, and in particular **photos or recordings of any child
other than your own**;
(d) screenshots of other people's messages or profiles (see section 13); and
(e) information about a child's lessons, progress, behaviour or family.

9.2 Teachers do not have access to Parents' phone numbers, e-mail addresses, location or payment information,
and must not ask for them. Parents must not ask Teachers for their personal contact details.

9.3 Share your own child's photo only through the child's profile or where the Academy asks for it. Do not post
images of other children.

## 10. Boundaries between Teachers, Parents and Students

10.1 **No private channels.** A Teacher and a Parent never have a private one-to-one chat in the Service. They
communicate only in the Family group or Student group, where Academy staff are present. Parents do not have
direct chats with other Parents. These rules are built into the Service, and you must not try to get around
them.

10.2 **Communication stays in the Academy's groups.** Teachers, Parents and staff must keep all communication
about a child and the child's lessons inside the Service and in the groups the Academy has set up. You must not
move it to another app, telephone, e-mail or social-media account.

10.3 **No social-media contact.** Teachers and staff must not contact, follow, add, befriend or message a
Student, or a Student's Parent, on social media or any other messaging service, and must not accept such
requests. Parents must not invite Teachers to do so.

10.4 **No meetings arranged through the Service.** No one may use the Service to arrange to meet a child in
person. Teachers and staff must not meet a Student in person in connection with the Academy, except where the
Academy has arranged it in writing and the Parent has agreed.

10.5 **Appropriate hours.** Messages and calls should be sent at reasonable times, taking into account the
recipient's time zone, and outside lesson times only where they are necessary. [TO CONFIRM: the Academy's
communication hours.]

10.6 **Lessons.** Live lessons are audio only and take place inside the Service. A Teacher must not ask a child

10.7 **A child's privacy in lessons.** Teachers must not ask a child personal questions unrelated to the
lesson, about the child's home, family, location or other personal matters.

## 11. Professional conduct of Teachers and staff

11.1 Teachers and staff represent the Academy. In addition to the Teacher Terms or Staff Terms, they must:
(a) be punctual, prepared and courteous, and give lessons as scheduled;
(b) use language, tone and examples suitable for children;
(c) treat every Student fairly, patiently and without favouritism;
(d) never use physical, verbal or emotional punishment, humiliation or threats;
(e) keep Students' and families' information confidential and use it only for the Academy's work;
(f) use access to accounts and data only within their role and permissions, and never for personal purposes;
(g) declare any conflict of interest, including any family or personal relationship with a Student or Parent;
(h) not accept gifts or benefits from families beyond what the Academy allows [TO CONFIRM: the Academy's gifts
rule]; and
(i) follow the Academy's instructions on safeguarding, data protection and communication.

11.2 Staff who act in a Parent's or Teacher's account during a support session must act only for the purpose
recorded for that session and within the actions the Service allows. Support sessions are described in the
Privacy Policy.

## 12. Child safeguarding: everyone's duty

12.1 The safety of children comes before every other consideration in the Service, including the continuity of
lessons.

12.2 **Teachers and staff must report** without delay any concern that a child may be at risk of harm, abuse,
neglect or exploitation, whether the concern arises in the Service or outside it. They must use the report
tool with the reason for a child's safety, or write to {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}, and must follow the Child Safety
and Parental Responsibility Policy. This internal duty is in addition to any personal legal duty a Teacher
or staff member has to report a concern to the authorities in their own country.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** Legal counsel to confirm the reporting duties that apply

12.3 **Parents are strongly encouraged to report** any behaviour that makes them concerned for their child or any
other child, however small it seems.

12.4 Anyone who receives a concern must not investigate it alone, confront the person concerned, promise a child
secrecy, or forward, copy or screenshot the content. They should report it, and leave the handling to the
Academy's designated staff.

12.5 If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services first, then tell the Academy.

## 13. Recording, screenshots and redistribution

13.1 No one may record, photograph, screenshot or redistribute a lesson or call without the Academy's prior
written permission. Teachers and staff may never record a lesson or call. Lessons are not recorded by the
Academy. The same applies to a whiteboard or conversation, or any part of one.

13.2 You must not take screenshots of other people's messages, profiles or children's photos, and must not
forward or publish them outside the Service, except to report a concern to the Academy or the authorities.

13.3 Lessons, lesson materials, whiteboards, recordings of pronunciation and other content provided by the
Academy are for the personal learning of the enrolled Student. You must not copy, sell, publish, upload,
share or redistribute them outside the Service without the Academy's written permission.

## 14. Honesty and proper use of the report and block tools

14.1 **No impersonation.** You must not pretend to be another person, a Teacher, a member of staff or the
Academy, and must not use another person's account. Only the Academy's official account speaks for the
Academy.

14.2 **Accurate information.** Information you give the Academy, including about yourself, your children and
any concern you report, must be true to the best of your knowledge.

14.3 **No false or malicious reports.** You must not knowingly make a false report, or use the report tool to
harass, intimidate or retaliate against anyone. An honest report that turns out to be mistaken is never a
breach of this Code.

14.4 **No misuse of blocking.** Blocking is for protecting yourself from unwanted contact. You must not use
blocking, or repeated blocking and unblocking, to disrupt a child's lessons, avoid the Academy's
administration or harass anyone.

14.5 **No retaliation.** You must not retaliate against anyone because you believe they reported you or blocked
you, or because they took part in an investigation.

## 15. Security and integrity of the Service

15.1 You must not:
(a) access, or attempt to access, another person's account, data or conversations;
(b) share your password or one-time codes, or let another adult use your account (a child may use the
Parent's device under supervision, section 2.2);
(c) bypass, or attempt to bypass, any limit, permission, block or safeguard in the Service, including the
communication rules in section 10 and the limits on reports and blocks;
(d) probe, scan or test the security of the Service, or interfere with its operation, except under a written
authorisation from the Academy;
(e) use automated means (bots, scripts or scrapers) to access the Service or collect information from it;
(f) reverse-engineer, copy or modify the apps, except where the law allows it; or
(g) upload or send malicious files or links.

15.2 If you discover a security weakness, tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and do not use or disclose it.

## 16. Respect for religious content

16.1 The Academy teaches the Quran and Islamic studies, and expects everyone to treat the Quran, the Prophet
Muhammad (peace be upon him), the prophets and religious content with reverence and respect.

16.2 Quranic text and recitation must be handled accurately. You must not deliberately alter, misquote or
distort the Quran, and must not use verses or religious texts in a mocking, offensive or inappropriate way.

16.3 Questions or disagreements about religious matters, including about what is taught in a lesson, should be
raised respectfully with the Academy, which will refer them to qualified staff. They should not be pursued in
argument with a Teacher, a Parent or a child.

16.4 You must not use the Service to promote a particular sect, school or group against others, to attack or
insult the beliefs of any person or group, to declare others to be unbelievers, or to invite Students or
families to doctrines or activities outside the Academy's curriculum.

16.5 Teachers teach the Academy's approved curriculum and do not add personal religious views or rulings beyond
it.

## 17. Summary of prohibited activities

Without limiting the sections above, you must not use the Service to:
(a) send or share objectionable content (section 6);
(b) sexualise, groom, exploit or abuse a child in any way (section 7);
(c) harass, bully, threaten, blackmail or abuse anyone (section 4);
(d) promote hatred or discrimination (section 5);
(e) send spam, run scams, or ask for or offer payment outside the Academy (section 8);
(f) share other people's personal information or images, especially of children (section 9);
(g) contact Students or Parents outside the Academy's groups or on social media, or arrange to meet a child
(section 10);
(h) record, screenshot or redistribute lessons, calls, conversations or materials (section 13);
(i) impersonate anyone, make false reports, or misuse the report or block tools (section 14);
(j) access other people's accounts or bypass the Service's limits and safeguards (section 15);
(k) disrespect or distort religious content, or engage in sectarian attacks or proselytising (section 16); or
(l) do anything unlawful, or encourage anyone else to break this Code.

## 18. Your responsibility for Content

18.1 You are responsible for the Content you send and for the Content sent from your account.

18.2 Messages you have sent remain visible to the other members of the conversation, and may be kept in the
Academy's records as described in the Privacy Policy, even after you delete your account.

## 19. Consequences of breaking this Code

19.1 When we find that this Code has been broken, we act in proportion to the seriousness of the breach, its
effect on others, in particular on children, and any earlier breaches. The measures, from the lightest to the
most serious, are:
(a) **removal of content**: the message or file is deleted for everyone in the conversation;
(b) **warning**: the person receives a conduct warning notice reminding them of this Code;
(c) **temporary restriction**: for example, assigning the Student to a different Teacher, or limiting the use
of certain features;
(d) **temporary suspension**: the account is suspended, all of its sessions are signed out, and the account
cannot be used until the Academy reactivates it; and
(e) **closure of the account**: the account is closed indefinitely and the Academy will not reactivate it or
accept a new enrolment from the same person.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** No terminal account state or sign-up deny-list exists in code;
> proposal P-6 (outstanding register).

19.2 We may combine measures, and we may move to a more serious measure when a breach is repeated.

19.3 **Severe cases**, including any breach of section 7, grooming, threats to a person's life or safety, and
abuse of a child, may lead directly to suspension or closure of the account and to referral to the competent
authorities. We may restrict an account immediately, pending investigation, when we believe a person is at risk.

19.4 For a Teacher or staff member, a breach of this Code may also be handled under the Teacher Terms or the
Staff Terms and the contract with the Academy.

19.5 The measures, the appeal procedure and the effect on lessons and payments are described in the Blocking,
Suspension and Enforcement Policy.

## 20. How to report

20.1 **In the app.** You can report any message you can see (except the Academy's own lesson cards and system
messages), and any person you share or shared a conversation with. Choose Report on the message or on the
person, select a reason, and send. Only the Academy's team sees your report. The person is not told.
Reports cannot be filed in the app about the Academy's official account, a department manager or the Super
Admin; those concerns go to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or, for child safety, {{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}} (Reporting and
Complaints Policy, section 3.5).

20.2 **Block.** You can also block a person who is bothering you. Lessons continue as usual, and the Academy is
told so that it can follow up.

20.3 **By e-mail.** You can write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Child-safety concerns can also be sent to
{{SAFEGUARDING_EMAIL}}.

20.4 **In an emergency**, call your local emergency services first.

20.5 The Reporting and Complaints Policy explains what happens after you report.

## 21. Changes to this Code

21.1 We may update this Code, for example to reflect changes in the Service or the law. We will tell you about
material changes in the app before they take effect and, where required, ask you to accept the new version
before you continue to send Content. The version and effective date are shown at the top of this Code.

21.2 Reading the Code, signing out and deleting your account never require acceptance of a new version.
