# Privacy Policy

**Version** {{VERSION}} · **Effective** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · **Language:** English · [العربية](privacy-policy-ar.md)

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Jawwid Academy collects, why, who receives it, where it is kept, how long it is kept, and what rights you have. It is a notice: you are not asked to accept it as a contract. It is always available in the app, in the teacher web portal and on the account deletion page.

## 1. Who we are

1.1 Jawwid Academy ("Jawwid", "the Academy", "we", "us") is operated by {{ENTITY_NAME}}, registered in {{ENTITY_COUNTRY}}, with its address at {{ENTITY_ADDRESS}}.

1.2 {{ENTITY_NAME}} is the **controller** of the personal data described in this Policy. That means it decides why and how the data is used and is responsible for it.

1.3 Our teachers and staff use personal data only on the Academy's behalf and under its rules.

1.4 We also use **service providers** (section 11). They handle data on our instructions to host the Service, deliver calls and notifications, take card payments, and report technical faults. We require our service providers, by contract, to protect personal data and use it only to provide their service to us [TO CONFIRM — contracts under review]. This does not apply where a provider has its own legal duties. For example, our card-payment provider has its own duties to prevent fraud and to follow card-network rules (section 11.3).

1.5 To contact us about privacy, write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. Our data protection officer can be reached at {{DPO_CONTACT}}.

## 2. Who this policy covers

2.1 This Policy covers personal data about:

(a) **Parents**: parents and legal guardians who hold a family account;

(b) **Children**: the learners a parent enrols with the Academy (Students). A child has no account of their own and uses the Service on the parent's device, under the parent's supervision;

(c) **Teachers** who teach for the Academy;

(d) **Staff** of the Academy, including supervisors, managers, administrators and the Super Admin;

(e) people who **request a trial lesson**; and

(f) people who use the public web pages of the Service, such as the account deletion page.

2.2 The **Service** means the Jawwid apps for Android and iOS, the teacher web portal, the Admin Web used by our staff, and the lessons we provide through them.

2.3 Records held by the Academy's separate enrolment and subscription systems are described in their own notice [TO CONFIRM]. Some information reaches the Service from those systems when a family enrols (section 4.2).

2.4 This Policy does not cover services that other companies run under their own name, such as the payment page of our card-payment provider. Their own privacy notices apply to what you give them directly.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** Whether this Policy also covers the jawwid.com website is [TO CONFIRM]. The target audience and the minimum age of an account holder ({{MIN_ACCOUNT_HOLDER_AGE}}, proposed 18) are owner decision LD-5.

## 3. Summary

> **The key points**
>
> - **The parent is in charge.** Children have no accounts. The Academy enrols each child at the parent's request, and the parent acts for the child in everything in this Policy (section 6).
> - **Lessons and calls are audio only and are not recorded.** Live audio is relayed and not stored. There is no video calling (section 4.8).
> - **No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.** We do not use advertising identifiers, GPS or device location, or cookies. We never sell personal data (sections 4.15 and 11.6).
> - **Teachers never see your contact details, location or payments.** Teachers and parents talk only in groups where academy staff are present.
> - **Your country and region** are typed by you, used for three stated purposes, and shared with no third party (section 4.3).
> - **Card details** are entered on our payment provider's page. We never see your card number. The provider does receive your child's name in the payment description (section 4.11).
> - **Staff support sessions** let an authorised staff member see the app as you see it, for at most 60 minutes, with a written reason and a permanent log. You are not notified today (section 9).
> - **Deleting your account** is immediate. Some records are kept, for example the messages you sent (shown as "Deleted user"), your children's records, and financial records (section 10).
> - **Your data is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.** Some service providers are in other countries, including the United States (section 11).
> - **You have rights** to access, correct and delete your data and to complain. Write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} (section 13).

## 4. Information we collect

### 4.1 Account and sign-in

4.1.1 For every account we hold: the sign-in name (for a parent, the parent's e-mail address); the account kind (family, teacher or staff); its status; the time of the last sign-in; the number of failed sign-in attempts; and the password, stored only as a one-way hash (argon2). We never store your password in readable form.

4.1.2 **How a parent's account is created.** The Academy creates the family account. When a family enrols, our sales team records the parent's e-mail address, and our staff enrol the children. The parent then **activates** the account in the app: the parent types that e-mail address, receives a 6-digit one-time code, and sets a password.

4.1.3 **How the activation code reaches you today.** The code is not yet sent automatically. The supervisor responsible for your family reads the code in our staff tools and sends it to you over WhatsApp. This means your phone number and the code pass through WhatsApp (section 11). A code stays valid until it is used or replaced by a new one. We limit how many codes can be requested and how many wrong codes can be entered.

4.1.4 **Account recovery and password reset.** One-time codes for recovery and password reset are sent to your e-mail address through our e-mail delivery provider. For parents, they may also be sent automatically to your phone number through WhatsApp, where that option is turned on [TO CONFIRM].

4.1.5 To slow down abuse, we keep a hash (a one-way code) of a sign-in name that fails to activate, for 7 days.

### 4.2 Parent and guardian information

4.2.1 **Profile:** your name, your relationship to the child, an optional photo, an optional "about" text, and your permissions within the family account. The family also has a display name and a language.

4.2.2 **Contact details held by staff** (phone, e-mail and region), with an internal staff note. The Academy records them when you enrol, and our staff may update them later. Only authorised staff can see them, within their department and the families they serve. Teachers never can.

4.2.3 **Family contact keys:** a keyed hash of each e-mail address and phone number in the family. We use it to match a new enrolment to a family that already exists, without comparing the readable details.

4.2.4 **Information from the Academy's enrolment systems:** when a family enrols or a subscription starts, the Academy's separate enrolment and subscription systems send the Service the details needed to set up the family and its lessons [TO CONFIRM].

4.2.5 **Trial requests:** if you ask for a trial lesson, we record your name, e-mail address and phone number; each child's name, level and a note; and your country.

4.2.6 **Time zone:** the time zone of your account, used to schedule lessons and show times correctly.

### 4.3 Parent location (country and region)

4.3.1 **What we collect.** Your country and your first-level region: your state, emirate, governorate or province. **We do not use GPS or your device's location, and the app does not ask for location permission.**

4.3.2 **How we collect it.** The app asks you once, in a prompt you can answer (**Save**) or postpone (**Later**). You type or choose the answer yourself. The country of your phone number is shown only as a suggestion.

4.3.3 **Why we use it.** For three purposes only:

(a) as part of your family's registration record;

(b) to address academy announcements (Stories) to the right audience, for example families in one country or region; and

(c) to read and show phone numbers in the right national format.

4.3.4 **Who can see it.** The Super Admin; Operations staff, for the families they serve; and the Sales team. **Teachers never see it, and other parents never see it** [TO CONFIRM].

4.3.5 **It is not shared with any third party.**

4.3.6 **How long we keep it.** When you delete your account, the location prompt and the region in the contact details held by staff are deleted. The country and region recorded for your family stay on the family record, which the Academy keeps with your children's records (section 10.3). A location given when booking a trial lesson is deleted 90 days after the trial if the trial did not lead to a subscription.

### 4.4 Children's information

4.4.1 We hold a child's name, level, assigned teacher, an optional photo, an optional "about" text, the child's date of birth or stated age, and the child's lesson records. Section 6 explains in full how we handle children's personal data.

### 4.5 Teacher information

4.5.1 For teachers we hold:

(a) contact and employment details: phone number, e-mail address, start date and teaching market;

(b) pay terms and payroll records;

(c) the payout account for paying the teacher, stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM);

(d) leave requests, including the free-text reason the teacher gives;

(e) supervision records: violations, suspensions and supervision notes;

(f) the teacher's professional profile: photo, biography, qualifications and certificate files; and

(g) the teacher's work in the Service: lessons taught, lesson reports written, whiteboards, messages and calls.

### 4.6 Staff information

4.6.1 For staff we hold: name, photo, role and department, job title, "about" text, presence and last activity (a staff member can hide "last seen"), and a record of the staff member's administrative actions in the audit log and of any support sessions (section 9).

### 4.7 Lessons, attendance, reports, rewards, certificates and whiteboards

4.7.1 For each lesson we keep: the booking, any move of a single lesson, apologies and make-up lessons; the outcome (for example held, or student absent) and minutes late; the lesson report and each revision of it; corrections; memorisation marks; practice; the interactive lesson materials opened; and the lesson balance of the family's lesson package.

4.7.2 **Rewards** are recorded as a permanent list of reward events.

4.7.3 **Certificates** record the name printed on them and the certificate file. A certificate is shown in an academy announcement (Story) only with a recorded consent.

4.7.4 **Whiteboards:** the teacher writes and the family views. We keep the page drawings, any images used on them, and a log of who opened each board.

### 4.8 Audio calls and lessons

4.8.1 Lessons and calls in the Service are **audio only**. There is no video calling. The app uses the microphone only for calls, lessons and voice notes you choose to record.

4.8.2 **Live audio is relayed in real time by our audio provider and is not recorded or stored by Jawwid.** The Service has no recording function.

4.8.3 To connect a call, the audio provider receives an internal account number (not your name or phone number), the display name shown in the call, the name of the call room, and the times people join and leave.

4.8.4 We keep a **call record**: who took part; when the call started, was answered and ended; its duration; its outcome; and when each person joined and left. We use it for call history, lesson attendance and safeguarding.

4.8.5 The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo or a video to send in a chat.

### 4.9 Chat messages, voice notes, uploaded files and edits

4.9.1 **How people can talk in the Service.** Teachers and parents talk only in Family and Student groups, where academy staff are present. A teacher and a parent never have a private direct conversation. Parents cannot message other parents. Finance, technical and academic staff never message families.

4.9.2 **What we keep:** the text and type of each message; who sent it and when; reactions; read receipts; stars; pins; and mentions.

4.9.3 **Edits:** when a message is edited, we keep every earlier version.

4.9.4 **Attachments:** photos, video files, voice notes, documents (PDF, office files, ZIP and text files) and their original file name, duration and dimensions. They are stored privately and opened through short-lived signed links.

4.9.5 **Deleting a message:** see section 10.9.

4.9.6 Academy staff may add internal notes to a conversation that only staff can see. These notes are personal data about you and are covered by your right of access (section 13).

### 4.10 Reports, blocks and feedback

4.10.1 **Reports:** who reported, who or what was reported, the reason, any note, as evidence a snapshot of the reported message's text and a reference to its attachments, and how the report was resolved. Reports are handled as described in the Reporting and Complaints Policy.

4.10.2 **Blocks:** who blocked whom and when. Each block is notified to the responsible manager.

4.10.3 **Feedback to the administration:** your message, its category and the Academy's response.

### 4.11 Payments

4.11.1 **What we keep:** payment requests (including the child's name, the payer's name and the amounts); payments, instalments, refunds, invoices and receipts; and receipts you upload for a bank transfer. Payments may also be made in cash.

4.11.2 **Card payments.** Card payments are made on the hosted payment page of our card-payment provider, Stripe. **You enter your card details, e-mail address and billing details directly with Stripe. We never see or store your card number.** We keep only the reference numbers Stripe gives us for the payment, the payment intent and any refund.

4.11.3 **What Stripe receives from us:** the amount; the currency; a line-item name made of the product name **and your child's name**; a description of the lesson period (for example "live one-to-one"); our internal reference numbers for the payment request; and the page language.

Whether to keep it (and disclose it, as above) or remove it is owner decision LD-8. If it is removed, delete "and your child's name" in clause 4.11.3 and the matching text in sections 6 and 11.

### 4.12 Device and push information

4.12.1 **Devices:** the platform, app version and device name of each device you sign in on.

4.12.2 **Push notifications:** a push token, the platform, whether it is a call-ring (VoIP) token, and the device language.

4.12.3 **Sign-in sessions:** a keyed hash of the IP address (we do not keep the readable address), the user agent, the app or portal used, whether "keep me signed in" was chosen, and the session times.

4.12.4 **Notification content:** by default, notifications on your lock screen do not show the names of senders or callers. You can turn names on in the app. The notifications you receive are also kept in your Notification Center.

4.12.5 **App lock:** if you turn on the app lock, your device checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. The app receives only a yes or a no; it never receives biometric data.

### 4.13 Diagnostics and logs

4.13.1 We use Sentry to find and fix errors and crashes.

(a) **From our servers**, Sentry receives the type of error, the kind of request, the kind of account, the result and a request number. It receives no names, e-mail addresses, IP addresses, message contents or request contents.

(b) **From the mobile app** (released versions only), Sentry receives the technical crash details, a one-way hash of your account number, your device model and operating system, and navigation breadcrumbs (the names of the screens visited before a crash). E-mail addresses, phone numbers and quoted text are removed before sending. No screenshots are taken.

4.13.2 **Server logs** record the kind of request, the kind of account and a request number. They do not contain IP addresses, account numbers, message contents or phone numbers.

### 4.14 Consent and acceptance records

4.14.1 We keep a record of:

(b) any consent to show a child's certificate in an academy announcement; and

(c) the acceptance of the Terms of Use, the Teacher Terms or the Staff Terms, and of the Code of Conduct, with the version, the time on our server, the app or portal used and its language [TO CONFIRM].

4.14.2 These records are evidence of what was agreed or acknowledged and when. They are kept after an account is deleted (section 10.3).

### 4.15 Information we do not collect

4.15.1 We do **not**:

(a) collect GPS or other device location;

(b) use advertising identifiers;

(c) use analytics or advertising tools, or track you across other apps or websites;

(d) show advertising;

(e) record lessons or calls, or offer video calls;

(f) read your contacts or address book; or

(g) use cookies (section 16).

6.11 **No marketing and no profiling.** Children are never targeted for marketing. We do not build profiles of children for any purpose other than teaching them. We show no advertising.

6.12 **Children's records after the parent leaves.** Today the child's profile, lesson records, lesson reports and rewards are kept as the Academy's records when the parent deletes the account, including when the last parent of the family deletes it (section 10.3).

## 7. Legal bases

7.1 We process personal data only where a legal basis allows it. Which bases are available depends on the law that applies to you. We rely on the bases below **as recognised by the applicable law**.

7.2 **The bases:**

(a) **Contract.** Processing needed to provide the Service and lessons you asked for, to employ and pay teachers, and to take steps you ask for before a contract.

(b) **Consent.** Where the law requires consent, including the explicit consent of a parent or guardian for a child's data and for sensitive data (section 6). You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

(c) **Legal obligation.** Where a law requires us to process or keep data, for example accounting, tax and child-protection duties, or answering a lawful request from an authority.

(d) **Legitimate interests.** Where the applicable law recognises this basis: our interest in keeping the Service secure, preventing abuse, keeping children safe, fixing faults, keeping evidence, and running the Academy, balanced against your rights. Some laws, for example the law of Oman, do not recognise this basis [legal review]. For people protected by those laws, we rely on another basis that the law does recognise, such as the contract or consent.

(e) **Vital interests.** To protect someone's life or physical safety, for example in a child-safety emergency.

## 9. Staff support sessions

9.1 **What a support session is.** A support session lets an authorised staff member help a parent or a teacher by seeing the app exactly as that person sees it, and by doing a limited set of things for them, for example correcting a profile or sending a message on their behalf. It is a support tool with its own restrictions. It is not ordinary staff access to an account.

9.2 **Who can open one.** Only active staff members, and only within their department and scope:

(a) staff of the Operations department, for parent accounts, within the families they serve or under a recorded access grant;

(b) Teachers-department staff, within the scope the Academy sets for them, for teacher accounts; and

(c) the Super Admin, for any parent or teacher account.

Sales staff cannot. **Staff accounts can never be opened this way**, and no one can open their own account.

9.3 **Authorisation.** The staff member must write a reason (up to 500 characters) every time. No second person approves the session. The account must be active.

9.4 **Time limit.** A session lasts 15 minutes at a time; the staff member may extend it in 15-minute steps, but it always ends no later than 60 minutes after it started. There is no refresh token. Each staff member can have only one live session. The staff member's access is checked again on every request.

9.5 **What the staff member sees.** Everything the person sees in the app, including the parent's sign-in e-mail address and the person's own list of reports and blocked people.

9.6 **What the staff member can do.** Only actions on a fixed list:

(a) edit the person's own profile and photo, and their children's names, "about" texts and photos;

(b) edit a teacher's own professional profile and certificates, and leave requests;

(c) upload files, and send messages and attachments;

(d) star a message, delete a message for the person only, and change chat preferences.

9.7 **What the staff member cannot do.** Everything else is refused, including:

(a) sending read receipts, reacting, pinning, editing messages, deleting messages for everyone, and forwarding;

(b) making or answering any call;

(c) writing lesson reports and booking lessons;

(d) creating chats;

(e) filing Reports, blocking or unblocking anyone, or sending feedback;

(f) accepting terms or acknowledging the lesson-audio notice; and

(g) connecting to live updates.

9.8 **Whether you are told.** Today you are **not** notified when a support session starts or ends, and no banner is shown to you. A message sent during a session is labelled **"Sent by support"** for everyone in the conversation, including you. The label does not name the staff member. A support session does not appear in your list of signed-in devices.

9.9 **The permanent record.** Every session is recorded permanently: the staff member, the account, the reason, and the start, expiry and end times. Every request made in the session is also recorded: its kind, the part of the Service it reached, and its result. **The contents are never recorded.** If the record cannot be written, the action is refused. Each action in our audit log carries the session it was made in.

9.10 **Attribution.** In our records, every action in a support session is attributed to the staff member and the session. To other users, messages appear from your account with the "Sent by support" label.

9.11 **Safeguards.**

(a) Sessions are limited by department, and by the families a staff member serves or the teachers within their scope.

(b) Staff accounts can never be opened.

(c) Sessions have a time limit and a fixed list of allowed actions.

(d) Every session and request is logged permanently.

(e) The Super Admin can see every session; other staff members see only their own.

> **Drafting note (remove before publication):** Today the user is not told about a support session, and no second person approves it. Whether to notify the user (for example in the Notification Center after each session), whether to add a second approval, and whether the Super Admin reviews the log regularly are owner decision LD-3. Section 9 states the current behaviour and must be updated if it changes. [legal review]

## 10. Retention and account deletion

### 10.1 How to delete your account

10.1.1 **In the app:** Settings → Delete account. You confirm with your password.

10.1.2 **On the web:** on our account deletion page at {{ACCOUNT_DELETION_URL}}, with your sign-in name and password. You do not need to install the app.

10.1.3 Parents and teachers can delete their own accounts. A staff account is closed by the Super Admin or the staff member's department manager (section 10.5).

10.1.4 Deletion is final and cannot be undone. Before you confirm, the app tells you what will be deleted and what will be kept.

10.1.5 If you cannot sign in, write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We will verify your identity before acting (section 13.4).

### 10.2 What is deleted immediately

10.2.1 Deletion takes effect at once, in a single operation. The following are deleted:

### 10.4 Teachers

10.4.1 A teacher who still has a future lesson, an assigned student, or this month's or last month's payroll open can request deletion in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on the web page in section 10.1.2. The app shows the date by which the account will be deleted. The Academy then moves the teacher's students and completes their payroll, and the account is deleted within 30 days of the request at the latest, even if something is still open. Until then the teacher can cancel the request in the app. If this month's or last month's payroll is still open when the account is deleted, we keep the teacher's bank details (payout account) until neither month has any of their lessons on record or any change to their pay. In practice, that is the end of the month after the last month with such a lesson or pay change. We then delete the bank details automatically. We do not wait for a record that the wages were paid, because our systems do not record that; our staff are given a task to pay any wages owed before then [legal review]. A teacher with nothing open is deleted at once.

10.4.2 When a teacher's account is deleted, we delete the teacher's phone number, e-mail address, professional profile, certificate files and payout account (the payout account at the time set out in section 10.4.1), and the Teachers department manager is told.

10.4.3 Pay and supervision records are kept (section 10.3). The whiteboards of a teacher who has left are deleted 12 months after the teacher leaves.
