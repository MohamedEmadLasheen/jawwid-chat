# PD-12 S2: the teacher discipline ladder

Record: `docs/product/JAWUID-CHAT-PRODUCT-BOUNDARY.md` §4 PD-12 §3.
Migration: `supabase/migrations/20261019140000_chat_pd012_s2_discipline.sql`.

## Violations

A violation is `chat.teacher_violation`: one row per lesson and kind.

- **The teacher's apology or absence.** A trigger on `chat.lesson` writes the row
  the moment a lesson closes `teacher_apology` or `teacher_no_show`, whoever
  closed it. That includes trial lessons; `learner_id` is never read. The academy's
  own cancellations (`admin_cancelled`, including those made for leave or a
  suspension) are never violations.
- **Lateness.** The Teachers department records it on a **held** lesson with
  `POST /lessons/:id/lateness {minutes, note?}`. It counts only from
  `teacher.late_threshold_minutes` (default 10). Below that, the request is refused.

The month a violation counts in is the lesson's calendar month in
`schedule.timezone` (`chat.academy_month`).

## The ladder

| Step | Suggested decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | warning |
| 2 | first deduction |
| 3 | second deduction |
| 4 | temporary suspension |
| 5 and beyond | permanent suspension |

The step is the violation's rank among the teacher's **counted** violations of the
same month: an excused violation keeps its place but is not counted, and the count
starts again on the first of every month. It is computed on every read (`ladder.ts`)
and never stored. Only the step that was suggested when a person decided is written,
alongside the decision.

## Deciding

`POST /discipline/violations/:id/decide {decision, note?, startsOn?, endsOn?, cancelCollidingLessons?}`.
The Teachers department (manager or member) and the super admin may decide. Each
violation is decided once. Choosing anything other than the suggestion, or
excusing, needs a `note`.

| Decision | Effect |
|---|---|
| `warning` | a `discipline_warning` card in the teacher's supervision conversation |
| `deduction_1` / `deduction_2` | a `discipline_deduction` card, and `teacher.deduction_applied {step, month}` to Core payroll. Since PD-26 the super admin's default amount for the step is written as its pay adjustment at once (`docs/admin/pd026-teacher-supervision.md`). |
| `temporary_suspension` | `chat.teacher_suspension` (default `teacher.suspension_default_days` = 7 days from today), one `teacher_day_off` row per day linked through `suspension_id`, so make-up booking skips them and they never count against leave; a card; `teacher.suspended` to Core/HR; open lessons in the window are cancelled for the families |
| `permanent_suspension` | refused while the teacher still has students: move them first with the Schedules tab's transfer. The open lessons and trials ahead are cancelled FIRST (with `cancelCollidingLessons`), while the teacher is still live -- a trial's cancellation card goes to the teacher's trial group, which needs one. If any cannot be cancelled, nothing is decided: 409 `DISCIPLINE_NOT_CANCELLED` with `detail.cancelledLessons` / `detail.notCancelled`, and the teacher stays active. Then one transaction, which refuses (409 `DISCIPLINE_LESSONS_COLLIDE`, decide again) over any lesson still open: a card, `teacher.suspended`, the teacher's login closed as PD-43's offboarding closes it (an active account through `chat.deactivate_principal('account')`, a provisioned or suspended one deactivated; every waiting first sign-in and reset code superseded, so none can activate it later; on the decision's audit row as `after.login`), and the teacher deactivated through `chat.deactivate_principal` (RT-032: signed out everywhere through the journal and its barrier). Fixed 2026-10-28 (found by PD-43): the teacher used to be deactivated first, so their trials could not be cancelled |
| `excused` | kept on record, no longer counted; no card |

If open lessons fall inside a suspension, the decision is refused with
`DISCIPLINE_LESSONS_COLLIDE` until `cancelCollidingLessons: true` is sent. Each
lesson is then cancelled through `LessonService.cancel`, so each family gets its
card.

A suspension day cannot be deleted from `teacher_day_off`, and a violation is never
edited or deleted (both are enforced by triggers).

## Reading

- `GET /discipline/violations?state=open|decided|all&month=YYYY-MM&teacherId=&kind=late|cancellations`:
  the "Needs decision" queue (`open`, oldest first), plus the Lateness and
  Cancellations lists.
- `GET /teachers/:id/discipline?month=`: one teacher's month. It returns the counted
  number, the next step, the violations and the suspensions.

## Known follow-ups

- Lessons are generated four weeks ahead. Skipping teacher days off during
  generation is in the lessons session's T4 migration (`20261019120000`), so a
  suspension or leave longer than the current four-week horizon is fully covered
  once T4 merges.
- Lifting a temporary suspension early is not built.
- The supervision timeline (#50) does not list violations yet. It should once #50
  and T4 have merged, because all three edit the same query.
