# PD-29 P0-4, Juz' Amma batch 4 (78–84, 86–88): lines held for teacher review

The last ten surahs of Juz' Amma, after batches 1–3 (`pd029-p04-batch1-review.md`, `-batch2-`, `-batch3-`) and Al-Buruj. The method and rules are the same.

- Each lesson's prose was copied **as text** from the old lesson (jawwid-lessons @ c231532, never built or run), byte-for-byte or as exact pieces joined.
- The Quran appears only as positions in the verified Tanzil text.
- Each file is **pending**: the lesson shows nothing from it until a Teachers department reviewer approves it, and a flagged line is never shown.

81 lines are held across the ten surahs. With this batch, every surah of Juz' Amma (78–114) has a reviewed file.

**Held for their substance, worth a teacher's first look:**

- 81 tafsir 19 says the Quran is "the speech of Jibril" (كلام جبريل). The Quran is Allah's speech, which Jibril conveyed; this must not be shown as written.
- 80 ('Abasa): five lines use «كشّر وجهه» or «لا تهتم به» of the Prophet ﷺ, which is harsher than tafsir. Tafsir says he frowned (قطّب/عبس), occupied with the Quraysh chiefs out of eagerness for their guidance. None was reworded.
- 79 tafsir 1–4 and its word card give only the angels for the opening oaths. Tafsir also reports death, the stars, souls, bows, ships and horses.
- 87 tafsir 11–13 widen the fate of al-ashqa (the disbeliever) to anyone who disobeys.

**Typed Quran.** Standalone typed Quran is held TYPED: quiz options, answers, and a lesson line that is only Quran words (78 q9, 79 q7, 82 q7, 83 q5/q8, 87 q8 and lesson 3). Short runs of an ayah's own words inside a tafsir **sentence** are paraphrase and are not held, as in batches 1–3. A teacher may still want to glance at the closest ones:

- 84 tafsir 22 (3 of the ayah's 4 words) and tafsir 25;
- 88 tafsir 7, 11, 18, 19 and 20.

Where a line *opened* with a whole ayah, it is CUT to `text: null` and held: 81 tafsir 9 and 26, 84 tafsir 21, and 88 tafsir 6 and 10. A teacher has to write those lines.

**The old Tajweed** was dropped in all ten, as in every batch.

**Read beyond the components.** Several lessons keep their lessons, reflection and did-you-know lines inside `TajweedSection.tsx` (78, 86). Some read `pages/Index.tsx` or `WelcomeSection.tsx` only to locate content. Every file read is in provenance.
