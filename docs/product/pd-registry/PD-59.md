Owner-approved stages: New Lead, Contacted, Trial Booked, Trial Completed, Converted, Lost. Sales owns
lead management, follow-ups, conversion tracking and the hand-over to Operations. Design of record:
`docs/admin/navigation-architecture.md` "Sales pipeline".

Engineering decisions (the lead's to review):
- **No second lead system.** A lead is a `chat.trial_prospect`. Trial Booked, Trial Completed, Converted and
  a Lost caused by a trial's outcome are DERIVED on read from the prospect's trial lessons, `trial_outcome`
  and the hand-over (`apps/api/src/sales-pipeline/pipeline-stage.ts`), never copied. Only New Lead,
  Contacted and Lost-before-a-trial are stored (`pipeline_stage`), with `lost_reason`, `last_contacted_at`,
  `next_follow_up_on`, `follow_up_note`; `converted_at` / `converted_family_id` record a hand-over made
  without a trial.
- **Contact is confined** (G-07, like `trial_subscription`): parent name, phone, WhatsApp number and e-mail
  live in `chat.sales_lead_contact`, no request-role grant, row-level security on, read and written only by
  `src/sales-pipeline` (`sales-pipeline-confinement.spec.ts`). It is never written into a parent or contact
  table before conversion.
- **Access** is decided on the server: the super admin and staff in the Sales department
  (`chat.sales_department_role`), the people `GET /lessons/access` calls `sales`. Not teachers, Operations,
  Finance or Teachers staff. List rows carry only whether a channel exists; the detail read returns the
  contact and is audited (`sales_lead.contact_viewed`); every change is audited by field name, never value.
- **Conversion never duplicates a parent.** The lead's e-mail and phone numbers are keyed with the PD-42
  contact key (`contactKeyHash`); a family that already holds one is LINKED (the lead's copy is then
  deleted); none -> the existing family intake is the hand-over, after which converting again links it.
- Retention of an unconverted lead's contact is an open policy question for the owner (see the PR).
