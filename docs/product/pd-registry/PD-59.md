Owner-approved stages: New Lead, Contacted, Trial Booked, Trial Completed, Converted, Lost. Sales owns
lead management, follow-ups, conversion tracking and the hand-over to Operations. Design of record:
`docs/admin/navigation-architecture.md` "Sales pipeline".

Engineering decisions (the lead's to review):
- **No second lead system.** A lead is a `chat.trial_prospect`. Trial Booked, Trial Completed, Converted and
  a Lost caused by a trial's outcome are DERIVED on read from the prospect's trial lessons, `trial_outcome`
  and the hand-over (`apps/api/src/sales-pipeline/pipeline-stage.ts`), never copied. Only New Lead,
  Contacted and Lost-before-a-trial are stored (`pipeline_stage`), with `lost_reason`, `last_contacted_at`,
  `next_follow_up_on`, `follow_up_note`; `converted_at` / `converted_family_id` record a hand-over made
  without a trial.
- **Contact is confined** (G-07, like `trial_subscription`): parent name, phone, WhatsApp number and e-mail
  live in `chat.sales_lead_contact`, no request-role grant, row-level security on, read and written only by
  `src/sales-pipeline` (`sales-pipeline-confinement.spec.ts`). It is never written into a parent or contact
  table before conversion.
- **Access** is decided on the server: the super admin and staff in the Sales department
  (`chat.sales_department_role`), the people `GET /lessons/access` calls `sales`. Not teachers, Operations,
  Finance or Teachers staff. List rows carry only whether a channel exists; the detail read returns the
  contact and is audited (`sales_lead.contact_viewed`); every change is audited by field name, never value.
- **Conversion never duplicates a parent.** The lead's e-mail and phone numbers are keyed with the PD-42
  contact key (`contactKeyHash`); a family that already holds one is LINKED (the lead's copy is then
  deleted); none -> the existing family intake is the hand-over, after which converting again links it.

Owner decisions (2026-11-21), built in this PD:

**Ownership.** Each lead has an owner (`trial_prospect.owner_staff_id`), a Sales representative. A representative lists, reads,
updates, contacts and converts only the leads assigned to them; the Sales manager reaches every lead of the academy and is the
only one who reassigns (`POST /sales-pipeline/leads/:id/owner`, audited `sales_lead.reassigned` with who, from, to, never a
contact value); the super admin has full access; Operations, Finance, the Teachers department and everyone else have none (403
before any lookup). A new lead is owned by its creator unless the manager names another. Enforced in the service on every read
path (list, counts, follow-up count, detail, the row lock of every write) and again by the database: a guard trigger refuses an
owner who is not a live member or manager of the Sales department (or the super admin) of the lead's organization. A
representative asking for another's lead gets the answer for a lead that does not exist (404, the same message, no contact read,
no audit row): no existence oracle. A prospect that existed before PD-59 has no owner and is reached by the manager and the super
admin until assigned. The connection runs as the table owner (see the PR), so row-level security cannot carry this; the service
scope and the guard trigger do.

**Retention: 12 months.** An unconverted lead whose last MEANINGFUL activity is more than 12 months old loses its personal data:
the confined contact row (parent name, phone, WhatsApp, e-mail) is deleted, and the free-text notes that could hold more are
cleared (the prospect's `note`, its `follow_up_note`, its students' notes). Stage, lost-reason code, timestamps, owner and counts
stay as analytics. *Meaningful activity* = the lead created; marked contacted; a stage change; a follow-up change; a contact
edit; a reassignment (each writes `last_activity_at`); and a trial booked or held for the lead (the later of its lesson's
creation and end). Reads and views, in a list or in the detail, are NOT activity. A converted lead is not purged (its contact
copy was deleted at the link). The job is `chat.purge_expired_sales_leads(limit, dry_run)`: it re-checks expiry itself, writes one
audit row per lead (`sales_lead.retention_purged`: id and what was cleared, never a value), and never touches a family, a
parent record, a contact, a learner or an account (a test reads the function and fails if it names one). The table guards allow
the deletion and the note clearing only while the purge runs. The worker runs it daily, **off unless
`SALES_LEAD_RETENTION_ENABLED=true` and a dry run unless `SALES_LEAD_RETENTION_DRY_RUN=false`**, `SALES_LEAD_RETENTION_BATCH`
(default 200, at most 1000). A lead's child display name (the label BR-2 keeps on every prospect and trial) is not cleared.
Not built: a lead that became Converted through a subscribed trial and was never linked keeps its contact copy until it is linked.

Review fixes (same day):
- **NULL owner.** A prospect with no owner is reached by the Sales manager and the super admin only. Prospects recorded outside
  the pipeline (`TrialService.createProspect`, a booking's own party) are owned by their creator when the creator is a Sales
  member or manager (not the super admin, Operations or the Teachers department, which leave it NULL); the migration backfills
  existing prospects the same way, idempotently, from `created_by` where that person is still live Sales staff of the same
  organization. An owner, once set, is changed to another owner and never cleared (a guard trigger).
- **Trial endpoints.** For a Sales REPRESENTATIVE (a Sales member who is not its manager, not the super admin, and not also on
  Operations or the Teachers department) `GET /trials/prospects` and the prospect lookup when booking a trial are scoped to the
  prospects assigned to them. The Sales manager, the super admin, Operations and the Teachers department read as before.
  The trial list and trial cards are not changed (they are the existing approved trial-desk rules).
- **Retention never touches a customer.** A lead is kept whole when it is converted, linked to a learner, has a subscribed
  trial, or has any enrolment record, whether or not Operations has enrolled it yet. Only an old unconverted, unsubscribed lead
  (a pre-PD-59 prospect falls back to `created_at`) is purged. The purge takes an advisory lock (one run at a time), locks each
  candidate `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and re-checks the expiry after the lock.
- A converted lead is not reassigned.
