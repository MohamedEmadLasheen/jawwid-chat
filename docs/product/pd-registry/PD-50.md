Picked when an excuse is recorded -- by the family (before the start or a late apology), the supervisor (student excuse before or after the start) or the teacher (teacher excuse). Cards stay write-once: the category travels in the payload of new cards; older cards have none. Follow-up to #289. Migration slot 20261030040000, assigned by the department head.

Department head review of #298 (2026-10-29): the owner's decision is about the family's own excuses. A teacher apology's category is staff data (a teacher's health is not the family's): stored on the lesson, returned to staff and the teacher only, never on the card or its body. A correction that changes the excused party (teacher <-> student) or leaves an excuse drops the category. A lesson is never inserted with a category.
