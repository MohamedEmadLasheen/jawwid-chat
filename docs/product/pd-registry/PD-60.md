The owner approved a dedicated Students page under People & Organization: student listing,
search and filtering, essential student information, a direct link to the existing student
profile (`/students/:learnerId`), server-side pagination and server-side permissions.

Engineering decisions (the lead's to review):
- **No migration, no new table.** Reads the existing `Learner`, `LearnerSuspension`,
  `LearnerScheduleSlot`, `LearnerSupervision`, `Family` and `Teacher` rows. No second student
  system: the profile is the existing `StudentProfilePage`.
- **Endpoint:** `GET /learners` (`@Requires('students.view')`, the key of
  `GET /learners/:id/profile`), in `JourneyController`, served by `StudentDirectoryService`.
  Query: `q`, `teacher` (id or `none`), `supervisor`, `status` (`active|paused|stopped`),
  `page` (1..10000), `limit` (1..100, default 25). Stable order `name, id`. Malformed input is a 400.
- **Who sees which rows = who may open the profile** (`SchedulingService.learnerFor` standings):
  the super admin (every learner), the Teachers department (learners of the teachers in its
  PD-35 scope) and a supervisor (the children they supervise). A teacher, a parent, an inactive
  account, and staff who are neither in the Teachers department nor supervise anyone (Sales,
  Operations without children) get 403. Access is not widened.
- **Essential fields only:** id, name, family id and display name, teacher, supervisor, status
  (`active|paused|stopped`, from the active suspension: temporary = paused, permanent = stopped),
  return date, count of weekly times. No parent contact, birth date or money.
- **Filter choices** (`filters.teachers`, `filters.supervisors`) come from the caller's own
  accessible set, so they leak no teacher or supervisor the caller could not already see.
- **Navigation:** new area `students`, offered on the server's Teachers-department word
  (`lessonManager`), under People -> Families beside Parents. A supervisor outside the department
  can call the endpoint and open profiles but is not offered the rail entry (UX only).
