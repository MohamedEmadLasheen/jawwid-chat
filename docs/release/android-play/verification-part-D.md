### Written question to Google Play Support (owner or counsel sends via Play Console > Help > Contact support; do not include secrets)

> Subject: Payments policy: 1:1 live lessons paid outside the app (package com.jawwid.jawwid_chat)
>
> Our app is free to download and will be distributed worldwide, category Education, audience includes children, parents and teachers. The app sells nothing. It uses no Google Play Billing and offers no in-app purchases and no in-app payment of any kind (card payments are disabled). Parents pay our academy for packages of live one-to-one voice lessons (one human teacher and one learner, in real time, never recorded and not replayable) by bank transfer or cash outside the app. The app only shows parents payment requests, invoices and receipts, and lets a parent upload a photo of a bank-transfer receipt.
>
> 1. Under the Payments policy, is Google Play's billing system required for this service? We note the help article "Understanding Google Play's Payments policy" (answer 10281818) states a 1:1 online paid service is exempt if it is between two individuals and not available for replay, with education classes as an example, but we could not find this wording in the policy text itself (answer 9858738). Which text governs, and does our service meet the exemption?
> 2. If the exemption applies, may the app display (a) the price of a lesson package, (b) payment requests and invoices, (c) bank-transfer instructions, and (d) a receipt upload, without breaching the rule on leading users to a non-Play payment method? If not, which of these must be removed?
> 3. If the exemption does not apply, which programme (alternative billing, external offers, user choice billing) is available in which countries for this use, and what is required?
> 4. Does a recorded or replayable element anywhere in the app (for example chat messages, voice notes, lesson summaries or homework audio sent outside the live call) affect the exemption?
> 5. Please confirm the answer in writing so we may cite it in our app review notes.

### Questions for counsel (COUNSEL-NEEDED)

1. After Google replies, is reliance on the 1:1 exemption reasonable in each major market (EEA, UK, US, India, Brazil, Gulf, others), and does any local consumer-protection or payment-services rule change that?
2. Do invoices, receipts, bank-transfer details and receipt upload in the app amount to "leading users to a payment method" under the policy as Google applies it? What wording keeps the display informational?
3. Does a "package" or "prepaid balance" keep the transaction a 1:1 service, or could it be treated as a digital good or subscription?
4. What are the consequences (suspension, removal) if Google disagrees after launch, and should launch in a smaller region set first?
5. Is the academy's collection of money from parents across borders (bank transfer, cash) in any country subject to licensing, tax or consumer-law disclosure that the app's display makes relevant?
